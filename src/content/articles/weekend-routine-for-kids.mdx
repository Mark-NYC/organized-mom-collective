By 10 on Saturday, the kids have asked what we’re doing four times, breakfast dishes are still out and Sunday night already feels like a scramble.

**A weekend routine for kids is six anchors in the same order each weekend: breakfast together, one family job each, free play, time outside, a family meal and a short Sunday-night reset.** Keep the order. Let the clock go.

Activities, birthday parties and practices slot in around the anchors. On a busy Saturday, the order still tells everyone what comes next.

<Tip label="Before you start" title="Agree on screens before Saturday morning.">

Decide together when weekend screens can start and when they end, then say it once on Friday. A rule set in advance is one fewer argument at 8 am.

</Tip>

## Weekend jobs kids can take

Give each child one family job that happens in the same place in the rhythm, every weekend. Ages come from our [chores by age guide](/resources/chores-for-kids-by-age).

<WhenLifeHappens title="When the weekend doesn’t cooperate">

**Packed Saturday?** Keep breakfast, one job and the Sunday-night reset. Skip the rest without guilt.

**Rain all day?** Swap time outside for an indoor game or a dance break.

**Weekend went sideways?** Do only the Sunday-night reset. It’s the anchor that protects Monday.

</WhenLifeHappens>
