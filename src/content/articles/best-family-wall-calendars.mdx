The 2027 calendars are out, and they all have boxes. What matters is which boxes your family will actually read.

## What’s the best wall calendar for a busy family?

**It depends on what your family needs to see.** Five people with their own activities: a calendar with a column per person, like Sandra Boynton’s My Family. The whole month with room to write: Amy Knapp’s Big Grid. One board you never rebuy: dry-erase. Dinners, groceries and chores next to the schedule: a weekly page, like ours. Plans that change by text all day: a Skylight display. No budget: Cozi’s free shared calendar. If what you have works, keep it.
