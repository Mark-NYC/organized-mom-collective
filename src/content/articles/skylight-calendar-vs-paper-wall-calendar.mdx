## Is a Skylight Calendar worth it, or is a paper wall calendar enough?

**A Skylight is worth it if your schedule lives in phones and changes all week.** It shows everyone’s synced calendars on the wall with no retyping, and its chore chart and lists are free. A paper wall calendar is enough if your week is fairly steady, the real problem is that nobody looks, and you’d rather write dinners by hand than pay $79 a year for Skylight’s meal planning. If your current calendar works, keep it.

## What’s free, what needs Plus, and what paper does

As of October 2026, from [Skylight’s comparison page](https://myskylight.com/compare-calendars) and its [Calendar Plus page](https://myskylight.com/products/calendar-skylight-plus). We haven’t tested a Skylight, and we make a paper calendar, so weigh that.

| Job | Skylight | Paper wall calendar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show everyone’s phone calendars | Free: syncs Google, Apple, Outlook, Cozi and Yahoo | Someone copies them by hand |
| Chore chart kids check off | Free | A paper chart |
| Grocery and to-do lists | Free | On the page, if it has them |
| Weekly meal plan | Calendar Plus | On the page, if it has one |
| Stars and rewards for chores | Calendar Plus | Stickers |
| Events from school emails | Calendar Plus | You write them in |
