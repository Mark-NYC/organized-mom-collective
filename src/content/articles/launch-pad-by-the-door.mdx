It’s time to leave. One shoe is under the couch, the library book is upstairs, and someone needs a signed form you haven’t seen. You’re ready to go, but the things you need aren’t.

**A launch pad is one place by the door for what leaves the house. Give bags, shoes and tomorrow’s extras a spot, then check it together the night before.**

## Give the essentials a home

Use the door you actually leave through. A hook, an existing basket or one end of a shelf is enough. Keep the walking path clear.

Start with three places:

- **Bags:** one hook or floor spot per person, within their reach.
- **Shoes:** a mat or basket for the pair going out tomorrow.
- **Extras:** a small tray for library books, signed forms or practice gear.

Give keys their own bowl or hook. Skip the whole shoe collection and last month’s papers. This spot holds the next departure, not everything the family owns.

It can sit beside [your home command center](/resources/home-command-center). The calendar tells you what’s happening; the launch pad holds what needs to go.

<RealLifeVersion title="A school-night example">

For two children, try two backpack hooks, two shoe spots and one shared tray. Label the bag spots with names so “put it away” means an actual place.

After dinner, take a quick look at tomorrow on [the family’s weekly plan](/resources/how-to-organize-your-familys-week). Each child puts their school things in their bag and returns the bag to its spot. An adult helps with what they haven’t learned yet and handles anything needing a signature.

Then check:

1. Backpack ready.
2. Shoes together.
3. Tomorrow’s extra item ready.

**Assign the evening check to a person, not automatically to mom.** Decide who handles it tonight. If lunch is still in the fridge, leave a reminder on the backpack; don’t put it by the door overnight.

</RealLifeVersion>

<WhenLifeHappens title="When the spot becomes a pile">

Keep only what goes out next. Return spare shoes and old papers to their homes.

Tiny entryway? Use one existing basket beside a shelf. If bags keep landing somewhere else, move the launch pad closer to that route.

Missed the evening check? Gather today’s essentials together. Restart tonight without a full-house organizing project.

</WhenLifeHappens>
