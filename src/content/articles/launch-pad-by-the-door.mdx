It’s time to leave. One shoe is under the couch, the library book is upstairs, and someone needs a signed form you haven’t seen.

**A launch pad is one place by the door for what leaves the house. Give each person’s bags, shoes and tomorrow’s extras a spot. Practice checking their own things the night before.**

Take a quick look at tomorrow on [the family’s weekly plan](/resources/how-to-organize-your-familys-week) first. **Agree on who owns the evening check, including remembering it.** If lunch is still in the fridge, leave a reminder on the backpack; don’t put it by the door overnight.

## Give the essentials a home

Use the door you actually leave through. A hook, an existing basket or one end of a shelf is enough. Keep the walking path clear.

It can sit beside [your home command center](/resources/home-command-center). The calendar tells you what’s happening; the launch pad holds what needs to go.

## A school-night example

For two children, try two backpack hooks, two shoe spots and one shared tray. Label the bag spots with names so “put it away” means an actual place. The adult responsible for the school check helps with unfamiliar steps and signatures; as children learn, they take over packing and checking their own things.

## Teach each person to check their own spot

1. **Set up the spot together.** Let your child place the bag and shoes where they can reach them. Give the job a clear finish: tomorrow’s essentials ready.
2. **Practice the three-item check.** Bag, shoes, extra item. Look at tomorrow’s plan together while they learn.
3. **Hand over the recurring check.** An agreed adult owns forms and unfamiliar needs; children take over their own packing as they’re ready. The adult sets their reminder rather than waiting for you to ask.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When the spot becomes a pile">

**Keep only what goes out next.** Return spare shoes and old papers to their homes.

**Tiny entryway?** Use one existing basket beside a shelf. If bags keep landing somewhere else, move the launch pad closer to that route.

**Missed the evening check?** Gather today’s essentials together. Restart tonight without a full-house organizing project.

</WhenLifeHappens>

## Make it part of your week

- **[An after-school drop zone](/resources/after-school-routine-for-kids):** the same spot in the afternoon, for unloading.
- **[Your child’s morning routine chart](/resources/morning-routine-chart-generator):** add “Grab backpack” so the launch pad is part of the morning.
