The gifts are open, paper is everywhere, somebody needs batteries and breakfast still hasn't happened. Christmas morning can become a day of managing everyone else's excitement.

**Make Christmas morning easier by preparing one room, agreeing on gift-opening expectations and sharing a few tiny jobs before the first present.**

<Tip label="Before bed" title="Keep your own traditions.">This isn't a minute-by-minute schedule. Make room for whatever your family values, whether that's reading the Christmas story, breakfast together or starting with stockings.</Tip>

## The cleanup rule that keeps the floor clear

**Put wrapping paper in the basket before moving to the next gift.** You don't need to sort every new toy or organize every bedroom before lunch.

<SplitList left={{label:'Do on Christmas morning',items:['Paper into one basket','Clear a safe walking path','Put dishes near the sink']}} right={{label:'Leave until later',items:['Sorting every new toy','Breaking down every box','Reorganizing closets']}} />

## End with a short family reset

When the last gift is open, everyone takes one small job. A parent can collect packaging while children put gifts in a safe spot and clear their own dishes. Then stop. The goal is to enjoy Christmas, not restore the house to showroom condition.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When the plan goes sideways">

**Someone needs a break?** Pause the gifts. The schedule can wait.

**Everybody's excited and the baskets are ignored?** Clear the floor together once, then carry on. Don't spend the morning enforcing a perfect system.

</WhenLifeHappens>
