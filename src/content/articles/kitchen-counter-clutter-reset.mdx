## Reset the counter in four categories

Use the downloadable sheet to check what is actually on your counter. You do not need to own every item on the list.

### Mailing and papers

Bills, flyers and invitations need different destinations. Recycle what is finished. Put records in one file spot. For anything requiring action, name the person and the next step. Our [school paperwork guide](/resources/school-paperwork-organization) helps with permission slips and notices.

### Utensils

Return spatulas, tongs and ladles to their drawer or crock. If the space is already full, move duplicates or rarely used utensils elsewhere rather than buying another organizer.

### Small appliances

Ask which items truly earn counter space. A coffee maker used every morning may stay. A blender used twice a month can go in a cabinet, if there is a safe, accessible place for it.

### Odd ends

Keys, change and elastic bands each need a specific home. A key hook or small tray is fine. Avoid creating a giant catch-all basket that simply hides the same clutter.

<Tip label="Before you start" title="One basket, one decision at a time.">

Take a basket for objects that belong in another room. Do not turn this into a cabinet cleanout. Keep sharp objects and private papers for an adult to handle.

</Tip>

## Make it a family job, not another job for Mom

Each family member can return their own things to their designated spot. An older child can sort unneeded flyers or put safe utensils away; an adult should handle private paperwork and sharp utensils.

Once the counter is clear, agree on a two-minute after-dinner check. The goal is a shared habit, not a spotless show kitchen.

If an item doesn't have an obvious destination, use our [second free sorting PDF](/downloads/family/kitchen-counter-clutter-reset.pdf). It walks through whether the item belongs elsewhere, needs action, needs filing or can leave.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When you only have five minutes">

**Clear one useful patch.** Start with the place where breakfast is prepared or lunches get packed.

**Something needs another person's decision?** Put it in a temporary action spot with an owner and a review time, rather than moving it to another surface.

**Miss a day?** Restart the normal two-minute reset. No catch-up marathon.

</WhenLifeHappens>
