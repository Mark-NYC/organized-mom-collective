The field-trip form is under yesterday's mail, picture day is coming, and nobody knows where the permission slip went. School papers need one destination before they become deadlines.

**Use one landing spot with three folders: ACT, SAVE and RECYCLE. Check the action folder each evening, and put every deadline on the shared family calendar.**

<ReferenceChart kicker="The paper station" title="Three folders, three decisions" columns={['Folder','What belongs here']} rows={[['ACT','Permission slips, forms to sign, notices that need a response'],['SAVE','Current school calendars, instructions and reference sheets'],['RECYCLE','Expired notices, duplicate papers and flyers you do not need']]}>Put the folders where backpacks are unpacked, not in a distant office.</ReferenceChart>

<Tip label="Before you start" title="Start with what you already have.">Three ordinary labeled folders in a file holder are enough. Keep a pen nearby. Set aside a separate box for keepsake artwork so it never blocks urgent papers.</Tip>

## The backpack-to-folder habit

When kids get home, backpacks land on their hooks, lunchboxes head to the kitchen, and papers go into ACT. An adult checks that folder after dinner, signs what needs signing, enters dates on the family calendar and returns forms to the backpack.

The child owns the first step. The adult owns the response. As children get older, they can help sort papers and flag anything with a deadline.

<SplitList left={{label:'Keep at the station',items:['Papers needing an action','Current school reference sheets','A pen and the family calendar nearby']}} right={{label:'Move somewhere else',items:['Artwork to keep for years','Old flyers and expired deadlines','Unsorted household mail']}} />

## Make the system work for two caregivers

Both adults check the same ACT folder and use the same calendar. If one signs a form, it immediately goes back into the backpack. Don't invent a second pile for papers that are almost done.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When papers pile up">

**Folder overflowing?** Remove expired notices first. Leave only current actions.

**A form is missing?** Search ACT, the backpack and school email in that order.

**Art keeps arriving?** Let each child select a few favorites once a month for a memory box.

</WhenLifeHappens>
