It’s 8:30, the kids are in bed, and the kitchen looks like dinner happened three times. Nothing is really dirty. Everything is just out.

Here’s the short answer: **the Daily Reset is five tasks, the same five every day, in about 15 minutes. When the timer goes off, stop.**

<Checklist kicker="Every day" title={dailyEssentials.title} time="15 min" icon={dailyEssentials.icon} items={dailyEssentials.tasks.map((t) => t.label)} />

It’s the daily half of a [weekly cleaning schedule built for real weeks](/resources/weekly-cleaning-schedule-for-busy-moms): this reset every day, plus one small zone Monday to Thursday.

<Shift title="A daily routine isn’t daily cleaning. It’s putting things back.">

Daily lists keep growing: sinks, toilets, floors, a load of laundry. Soon it’s most of an hour, and the first busy night ends it. **The reset only puts the house back to its starting line.** The scrubbing waits for its zone day.

</Shift>

<RealLifeVersion title="How to do the Daily Reset">

Split it: five minutes in the morning, ten after dinner. Keep a cloth, a spray bottle and a brush together under the sink, so the ten minutes doesn’t start with a search.

<Figure
  image="hc-02-caddy"
  alt="A cream cleaning caddy on a kitchen counter holding a glass spray bottle, folded green and white cloths and a wooden scrub brush."
/>

**Morning, 5 minutes**

1. **Make the beds.** Covers up, pillows back. That’s it.
2. **Unload the dishwasher.** So today’s dishes go straight in, not into the sink.

**After dinner, 10 minutes**

3. **Load the dishwasher and run it.** Kitchen first: counters can’t clear until the dishes have somewhere to go.
4. **Clear and wipe the counters and table.**
5. **Do a quick pickup.** Living room and entry only. One basket, one lap.
6. **Wipe obvious spills.** Whatever you can see standing up.

Only have time for one half? Do the evening. Kids can take one line each, the same line every night.

</RealLifeVersion>

<WhenLifeHappens title="When you can’t do the whole reset">

**Missed last night? Don’t do two tonight.** Just do tonight’s.

**On a hard night, shrink it.** Load the dishwasher and clear the counters. Five minutes, and tomorrow morning still starts clean.

</WhenLifeHappens>
