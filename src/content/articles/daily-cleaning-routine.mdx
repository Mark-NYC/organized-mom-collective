It’s 8:30. The school run is done, the beds are unmade and breakfast is still on the counter. Nothing is really dirty. Everything is just out.

**The Daily Reset is five tasks, the same five every day, in one 15-minute block. Agree on who takes each part, work through the list and stop when the timer goes off.**

It’s the daily half of a [weekly cleaning schedule built for real weeks](/resources/weekly-cleaning-schedule-for-busy-moms): this reset every day, plus [one small zone](/resources/zone-cleaning-schedule) Monday to Thursday.

<Tip label="Before you start" title="Pick the time, keep the kit together.">

After breakfast works for some families; after dinner fits others. Keep a cloth, a spray bottle and a brush together under the sink, so the 15 minutes doesn’t start with a search.

## Hand over part of the daily reset

Keep the same order and the same owners every day. **An adult can own dishes, another takes counters, and children take the parts they’ve been taught.**

<WhoCanHelp rows={[
  { job: 'Make beds', who: 'Ages 4–5', detail: 'Pull up the covers. From about 6, make their own bed.' },
  { job: 'The dishwasher', who: 'Ages 6–8 with you nearby', detail: 'Unload it while you put away sharp knives. From 9–12, load or unload on their own.' },
  { job: 'Counters and table', who: 'Ages 6+', detail: 'Set and clear the table. An adult can own the wipe-down.' },
  { job: 'Quick pickup', who: 'Ages 2+', detail: 'Toys back in their bin and their own things back where they live.' },
  { job: 'Wipe spills', who: 'Ages 2–3 with help', detail: 'Small spills with a damp cloth.' },
]} />

1. **Choose recurring parts together.** Include the time and what counts as done.
2. **Teach the unfamiliar part.** Walk through it once. Children need an age-appropriate job, not an adult’s whole checklist.
3. **Move the prompt off you.** Each adult uses their own reminder; children practice checking the list. Review what needs adjusting at the weekly check-in instead of directing every reset.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When you can’t do the whole reset">

**Missed yesterday? Don’t do two today.** Just do today’s.

**On a hard day, shrink it.** The dishwasher and the counters. Five minutes, and the kitchen still works tomorrow. If you can see the hard day coming, [plan it as a zero night for dinner](/resources/weekly-meal-planning-for-families#the-real-life-version): leftovers or takeout leave less to clean.

</WhenLifeHappens>
