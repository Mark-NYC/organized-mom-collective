It’s 5 pm and someone asks what’s for dinner. You decide. Again. On Sunday, your family can make those decisions together instead.

**On Sunday, spend 20 minutes checking the family calendar, choosing four or five dinners that fit, shopping your kitchen and agreeing on who owns each meal and the grocery list.** Put the plan where everyone can see it.

It’s the dinner part of [planning the family’s week once, on Sunday](/resources/how-to-organize-your-familys-week). Start from [the family calendar](/resources/how-to-organize-a-family-calendar).

<Shift title="Plan the nights before the food.">

Most meal plans start with recipes and assume seven nights of cooking. One late practice and the plan is over. **Sort the nights first, then pick dinners that fit them.**

</Shift>

## Shop your kitchen before making the list

Start with the fridge, freezer and pantry. Use what needs eating first, then write down only missing ingredients. Thursday’s [fridge check](/resources/weekly-cleaning-schedule-for-busy-moms#the-real-life-version) is a handy head start; [turn the meals into a grocery list](/resources/grocery-list-from-meal-plan) that another adult can shop from.

## Make dinner a shared family job

The person who owns a dinner checks ingredients, prepares it and adjusts the plan when the evening changes. They shouldn't need Mom to manage each step. Give children a repeatable part they can learn, such as setting the table, washing produce with supervision or gathering ingredients. See [age-appropriate dinner jobs](/resources/kids-helping-with-dinner).

**Try this on Sunday:** “Which dinner will you take this week, and which part can the kids own?” Put names beside the meals so the plan doesn't live in one person's head.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When the week changes">

**Swap, don’t scrap.** The person handling that dinner can move it to the next suitable night and update the plan. Agree on a swap if responsibility needs to change.

**Hard week?** Zero nights count. Choose leftovers or an easy pantry meal, and keep the job shared. A simple dinner also means [less to clean afterward](/resources/daily-cleaning-routine).

**Missed Sunday?** Take five minutes tonight to plan the next three dinners. No need to catch up on all seven days.
