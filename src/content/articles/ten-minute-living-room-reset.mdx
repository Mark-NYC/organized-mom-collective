The sofa cushions are on the floor. Someone left cups on the coffee table, and toys are spread across the rug. You don't need to deep-clean the living room. You just want it usable again.

**Set a 10-minute timer: collect dishes, put toys away, straighten the sofa, wipe the main table and clear the walking path. Give everyone one job and stop when the timer ends.**

<Tip label="Before you start" title="Give each person a finish line.">

Put one basket near the doorway and choose who owns dishes, toys and cushions. Children can take their own belongings; adults handle sharp objects, cleaning chemicals and heavy furniture.

</Tip>

## What counts as done in 10 minutes?

The room should be **safe to walk through, comfortable to sit in, and ready to use**. Not photo-ready.

The goal isn't to make Mom the cleanup supervisor. Practice each job once, then let its owner notice and finish it. [Choose age-appropriate responsibilities](/resources/chores-for-kids-by-age) if you're still figuring out who can do what.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When the room is more than 10 minutes away">

**Only have three minutes?** Clear dishes, pick up tripping hazards and put the biggest toys in their bins.

**Children resist?** Start together with one small, specific job each. Skip the lecture and revisit the routine when everyone is calmer.

**Missed yesterday?** Don't make tonight a 20-minute reset. Start with today's ten minutes and [leave the missed day behind](/resources/missed-cleaning-day).

</WhenLifeHappens>
