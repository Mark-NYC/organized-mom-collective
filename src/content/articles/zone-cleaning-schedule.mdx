You split the house into zones, and Monday’s zone was the whole downstairs. It took an hour and a half. By Wednesday you’d stopped.

**Split the rooms you use every week into four zones that each take 10 to 15 minutes, agree on an owner and a weekday for each, Monday to Thursday, and stop when the time is up.**

Rooms you don’t use every week, and the scrubbing, go into [a monthly home project](/resources/monthly-deep-cleaning-schedule).

<Shift title="A zone is a time limit, not a room.">

Most zone plans start with the floor plan: upstairs, downstairs, kitchen. Then each zone takes as long as it takes. **Size each zone to 10 or 15 minutes instead.** Big rooms get split or trimmed; small spots get combined.

## When a zone doesn’t fit

Change the zone, not the timer.

| If a zone… | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Runs past 15 minutes | Cut it to the four tasks that matter most, or split it over two weeks |
| Takes under 5 minutes | Join it to the room next door |
| Never happens on its day | Move it to a lighter day for good |

Here’s how [our weekly cleaning schedule](/resources/weekly-cleaning-schedule-for-busy-moms#the-real-life-version) sizes its four zones:

| Day | Zone | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Living Room | 10 min |
| Tuesday | Bedrooms | 10 min |
| Wednesday | Entry/Bathroom | 10–15 min |
| Thursday | Kitchen Reset | 15 min |

Wednesday combines the entry and bathroom. That fits some homes; the size and number of bathrooms may mean you need to trim or split the work. Two bathrooms or a playroom? Same rule: join it to a neighbor, or give it the day of the smallest zone.

Zones stay small because the [15-minute Daily Reset](/resources/daily-cleaning-routine) handles the dishes, counters and pickup every day. The zone only does what the reset skips.

## Teach the zone, then let someone own it

1. **Agree on a zone that fits their week.** Keep adult responsibilities realistic; give children a small taught job within the zone.
2. **Show the list and supplies.** Agree on the few tasks that count as finished. Let the person try before stepping in.
3. **Hand over the day’s reminder.** The owner checks the calendar and sets their own alert if needed. Review the arrangement weekly, and let their method differ when it meets the agreed finish.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When a zone day gets skipped">

**Skip it, don’t stack it.** Tomorrow is still tomorrow’s zone. The missed one comes back next week, or on the weekend if you want it.

**Ran out of time halfway?** Stop anyway. Next week, start that zone at the task you didn’t reach.

</WhenLifeHappens>
