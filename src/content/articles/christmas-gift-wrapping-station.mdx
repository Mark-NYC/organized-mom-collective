The tape has disappeared again. Wrapping paper covers the table, and the gift tags are somewhere in a bag from last year.

**A Christmas gift wrapping station needs just three zones: paper, small supplies and a clear surface. Keep them together, then give each person one job.**

  {title:'Agree on the reset',minutes:4,how:'Label each present, put supplies back and clear the surface before leaving.'},

<Tip label="Before you start" title="Separate surprise gifts.">Keep gifts that need to stay secret in an adult-only spot. Children can wrap gifts for relatives or friends without seeing their own surprises. Adults supervise cutting tools.</Tip>

## What makes a station useful

A pretty cart is optional. **Being able to find everything and finish without a mess is the whole point.**

<SplitList left={{label:'Keep within reach',items:['A few paper rolls and gift bags','Tape, tags and pens together','A bag for scraps']}} right={{label:'Skip for now',items:['A dedicated wrapping room','Every ribbon and paper you own','Decorations that block the work surface']}} />

## Wrapping jobs kids can own

Give children one complete task instead of making them wait for instructions at every step.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When wrapping takes over">

**Only have five minutes?** Put the tape and tags back in their box and stack the rolls. Finish the gifts another night.

**No space for a station?** A portable basket counts. Take it out when needed and put it back afterward.
