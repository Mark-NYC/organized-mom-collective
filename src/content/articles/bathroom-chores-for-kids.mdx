The towel is on the floor again. The toothbrush cup is beside the sink. You could fix both in ten seconds, but tomorrow they'll be there again.

**Give each child one small bathroom job, show them how, practice it together, then make it theirs.** Start with the towel, toothbrush cup or a quick wipe, not a whole bathroom to clean.

<Tip label="Good to know" title="Adults handle cleaning products.">

Keep cleaning products out of children's reach, and never mix cleaning chemicals. Younger children can use a damp cloth for water spots. An older child's cleaning job still needs an adult to choose the product and teach safe use.

</Tip>

<Shift title="Helping isn't the same as owning.">

A child who wipes the counter when asked is helping. A child who notices it's their turn and wipes it without waiting for you is learning ownership. **Teach the job and its reminder together.** Tie it to brushing teeth or leaving the bathroom, and keep a picture or short checklist where they can see it.

</Shift>

## Bathroom chores for kids by age

These are starting points, not deadlines. The same three jobs grow with your child: **hang the towel, return the toothbrush and cup, wipe the sink and counter.** "Help" means an adult stays involved; "own" means the child can finish after practice.

| Age | Can own after practice | Do together or with help |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2–3** | Put the toothbrush cup on a low, safe shelf; hang a small towel on a reachable hook | Guide their hand to wipe a water spot on the sink or counter with a damp cloth; help return the toothbrush |
| **4–5** | Hang their towel; put toothbrush and cup back in their places | Wipe a small sink or counter area with a damp cloth while you check the finish |
| **6–8** | Hang towel and return toothbrush and cup as part of the morning and bedtime routine | Wipe the bathroom sink and counter with you nearby, as in our age-by-age chore guide |
| **9–12** | Hang towel, return toothbrush and cup, and wipe the sink and counter with a suitable cloth | Clean the toilet or tub only with an adult choosing and supervising the cleaner |

<WhenLifeHappens title="When the towel lands on the floor again">

**Towel on the floor?** Practice the hook again after the next handwash. Don't turn one miss into a lecture.

**Siblings arguing?** Give each child a named hook or separate job so ownership is obvious.

**Busy week?** Keep just one job: towel on hook. Restart the others when the week settles.

</WhenLifeHappens>
