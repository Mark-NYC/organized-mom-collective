It's Wednesday, the sink has toothpaste on it, the hand towel is on the floor, and the mirror is covered in spots. You have a few minutes, not an afternoon.

**Here is the whole bathroom reset: clear the counter, wipe the sink, clean the mirror, clean the toilet, replace the towels and supplies, and sweep the floor. Set a 15-minute timer and stop there.**

<Shift title="A reset isn't a deep clean.">

If you start on grout, cabinet drawers or the shower curtain, you've taken on another project. **A reset only takes care of what makes the room usable today.** Save the bigger jobs for a separate cleaning block.

</Shift>

<RealLifeVersion title="How to finish the bathroom reset in 15 minutes">

1. **Take one lap with a basket.** Put away brushes, skincare and stray items. Don't open drawers to reorganize them.
2. **Start with the sink and mirror.** Use a cleaner that's suitable for each surface and wipe the visible toothpaste and water spots.
<Figure
  image="hc-04-window-cleaning"
  alt="A person wiping glass clean with a cloth in natural daylight."
  caption="Keep glass-cleaning jobs small. The bathroom mirror is enough for this reset."
/>

3. **Clean the toilet.** Scrub the bowl. Wipe or disinfect the seat, handle and outside with a separate cloth, following product directions and required contact time.
4. **Reset what people use.** Put up a clean hand towel, restock toilet paper and empty the bin.
5. **Finish at the floor.** Pick up what doesn't belong, then sweep visible debris or spot-clean a spill.

Keep cleaning products out of children's reach, and never mix cleaning chemicals. If someone else can take the towels and trash, **hand off that whole job**, including remembering when it needs doing.

</RealLifeVersion>

## What you can skip today

Skip shower grout, baseboards, cabinet organizing, the shower curtain and the full floor mop. Those are deep-cleaning jobs, not a quick bathroom reset.

If the room needs more work, give it its own spot in your [weekly cleaning schedule](/resources/weekly-cleaning-schedule-for-busy-moms). Our [zone-cleaning method](/resources/zone-cleaning-schedule) keeps one room from swallowing the rest of your day.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When you only have five minutes">

**Just do the sink, toilet and hand towel.** Put away the visible clutter, wipe the most-used surfaces and leave the rest for your next zone day.

**Missed the bathroom yesterday?** Don't double today's work. Restart with the same six jobs next time.

</WhenLifeHappens>
