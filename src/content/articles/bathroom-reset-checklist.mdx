Toothpaste on the sink, the towel on the floor, spots on the mirror. You have fifteen minutes, not an afternoon.

**The whole bathroom reset is six small jobs: counter, sink, mirror, toilet, towels and supplies, floor. Set a 15-minute timer and stop when it rings.**

<Tip label="Before you start" title="One basket, two cloths.">

The basket carries out whatever lives in another room. Use one cloth for the sink and mirror and a separate one for the toilet. **Keep cleaning products out of children's reach, and never mix cleaning chemicals.**

</Tip>

## What you can skip today

A reset isn't a deep clean. **It only takes care of what makes the room usable today.** If you start on the grout, you've taken on another project.

## Bathroom jobs kids can take

You don't have to do all six. **Hand one whole job to a child, including remembering when it needs doing.** Adults choose and handle the cleaning products.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When you only have five minutes">

**Just do the sink, toilet and hand towel.** Put away the visible clutter, wipe the most-used surfaces and leave the rest for your next zone day.

**Missed the bathroom yesterday?** Don't double today's work. Restart with the same six jobs next time.

</WhenLifeHappens>
