You’ve picked the week’s dinners. At the store, “tacos” is on the list, but you can’t remember whether there are tortillas at home. Whoever shops ends up texting whoever remembers.

Here’s how to make a grocery list from your meal plan: **write the ingredients and quantities, subtract what you already have, add everyday staples, and group what’s left by store section.** Keep one list everyone can add to.

Still choosing dinners? Start with [the weekly meal planning method](/resources/weekly-meal-planning-for-families). This is the shopping step.

<Shift title="Write a list someone else can shop from.">

“Yogurt” makes sense when you know the usual tub. Someone else may need the size, flavor and an acceptable substitute. **Put the decisions on the list.** The person shopping can then finish the trip without needing you on call in the dairy aisle.

</Shift>

<RealLifeVersion title="Turn the meal plan into a grocery list">

1. **List ingredients for the meals you’ve chosen.** Check recipes for portions. Include sides and toppings you actually plan to serve. Combine repeats: 1 pepper for tacos plus 2 for pasta becomes 3 peppers.
2. **Check the kitchen before adding more.** Look in the fridge, freezer and pantry. Cross off covered ingredients; reduce quantities when you have some. A child can count apples or check the cereal with you while learning this step.
3. **Add breakfast, lunch and regular extras.** Check milk, bread, fruit and lunchbox supplies. Keep a short staples checklist to review, rather than automatically buying it all again.
4. **Group by store section.** Produce, bread, pantry, dairy, meat and frozen is a starting point. Adjust the order to your store. Write amounts and any important dietary requirements beside the item.
5. **Agree on who owns the trip.** That adult checks the cupboards, finishes the list and shops. Everyone adds things when they use the last of them. Use one paper list or shared note; photograph a paper list before leaving.

## Example: three dinners, one combined list

Suppose your chosen meals call for 3 bell peppers and 12 tortillas across the week. You find 1 pepper and 4 tortillas at home.

| Store section | Add to the list |
| --- | --- |
| Produce | 2 bell peppers |
| Bread / tortillas | 8 more tortillas; buy 1 pack of 8 |
| Dairy | Plain yogurt, 32 oz; any brand |

This shows part of the list, not all the ingredients for three dinners. Keep the meals beside it using the [free weekly dinner planner](/meal-planner), or fit the check into your [family’s weekly plan](/resources/how-to-organize-your-familys-week).

<WhenLifeHappens title="When the shopping plan changes">

**No time for the full week?** List what you need for the next 2 dinners, plus breakfast and lunch basics.

**Something’s sold out?** Note an acceptable swap beside anything specific. The shopper chooses within those limits and updates the meal plan if dinner changes.

</WhenLifeHappens>
