For example, Tuesday might read: **“3:15 pickup, Mia with Dad; 5:00 soccer; pasta for dinner, Mom cooks.”** One line answers three questions before anyone asks them.

Picking the dinners takes longer than three minutes? Use [the 20-minute meal planning method](/resources/weekly-meal-planning-for-families) first. Hang the printed page in [a small home command center](/resources/home-command-center), beside the papers that need attention.

<WhenLifeHappens title="When your week changes">

**A practice gets canceled?** Cross it out. Write the change on the copy everyone uses.

**The week is already packed?** Fill in the appointments, dinner shortcuts and one important to-do. Leave the rest blank.

**You miss Sunday planning?** Start Wednesday. A useful half-week beats waiting for a perfect Monday.

</WhenLifeHappens>
