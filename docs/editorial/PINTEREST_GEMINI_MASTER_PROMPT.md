# Reusable request for Gemini pin-image sessions

Copy **one image concept at a time** from the batch prepared through [PINTEREST_GEMINI_WORKFLOW.md](PINTEREST_GEMINI_WORKFLOW.md). This setup message can precede the specific prompt.

> You are designing original Pinterest editorial imagery for Organized Mom Collective, a family-organization brand whose promise is that Mom should not have to manage everything alone. An organized mom raises an organized family: plans are visible and family members learn to take part.
>
> Make one vertical 2:3 Pinterest pin for the attached concept. Match the exact intended article and its promise. Prefer warm, lived-in family-home art direction with natural light, believable object scale and sophisticated magazine-quality composition. Do not repeat an existing pin's headline, subject or layout. Choose visual storytelling before decoration. Photography and headline must reinforce the same one idea.
>
> Set the headline **exactly once**, spelling and punctuation correct and easy to read at phone size. Use restrained OMC branding. No extra headings, unasked-for text, generic Canva blocks, made-up charts, fictional product UI, misleading testimonials or fake printed products. Keep natural object physics, clean typography and realistic hands. Under our current production QA, **avoid visible AI-generated faces**. If a verified real product is required, request the actual asset rather than inventing its appearance.
>
> For a correction, preserve everything that already works and edit only the specified defect. Never silently redesign an approved composition.

**Then paste the complete concept-specific prompt.**

Important: Gemini output must still pass [PINTEREST_QA_CHECKLIST.md](PINTEREST_QA_CHECKLIST.md), and actual original product imagery must follow [PINTEREST_PRODUCTION.md](PINTEREST_PRODUCTION.md). Gemini may not always render exact text or dimensions; visually inspect the exported image.
