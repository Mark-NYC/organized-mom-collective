**Daily 10-pin requests:** Use [PINTEREST_DAILY_PRODUCTION_DESK.md](PINTEREST_DAILY_PRODUCTION_DESK.md) as the production selector and output contract. Its standalone prompt requirements, exact hex palette, filename and season fields override the exploratory six-prompt default for this specific morning request. ## Authority
Read [PINTEREST_STRATEGY.md](PINTEREST_STRATEGY.md), [PINTEREST_PRODUCTION.md](PINTEREST_PRODUCTION.md), [PINTEREST_CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md](PINTEREST_CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md), [PINTEREST_QA_CHECKLIST.md](PINTEREST_QA_CHECKLIST.md), [BRAND_VOICE.md](BRAND_VOICE.md), current brand guidance, and the actual article before working. Those rules take precedence. This is the **Gemini image-generation route**, not a replacement for the established per-article three-concept, six-variation production contract. Cross-article six-prompt exploratory batches are ideation; do not label them completed six-variation production packages.

## Standard request: "Give me the next Gemini Pinterest batch"
1. Inspect GitHub's published articles and existing per-article Pinterest plans plus [PINTEREST_PIN_INVENTORY.md](PINTEREST_PIN_INVENTORY.md). Confirm actual target articles and exact slugs by reading source files. Verify live URL separately; disclose if unverified.
2. Choose six **distinct, underused articles or genuinely different supported angles**. Prefer seasonal opportunities only with grounded timing. Avoid repeating previously approved hooks, near-identical scenes and composition.
3. For every concept, record article URL, section/evidence, search/curiosity/save job, core promise, exact headline, visual subject/action, visual format and reason to click. Ensure the destination fulfills the headline. When preparing a production batch for one article, follow the existing six-variation approval gate.
4. Produce six standalone prompts for **Google Gemini**, following [PINTEREST_GEMINI_PROMPT_TEMPLATE.md](PINTEREST_GEMINI_PROMPT_TEMPLATE.md). Seek meaningful variety: editorial still life, close-up action, object-focused photography, graphic editorial treatment, overhead tabletop, original supplied photography. These are possibilities, not fixed slots.
5. Present the prompts and URLs for approval **before producing or publishing**. After images return, review actual full-resolution outputs against the existing QA checklist. Label each **approve / revise / reject** with concrete explanation. Preserve strong portions and request precise, local changes.
6. Only after approval, prepare title, natural-language description, factual alt text of the **actual finished image**, exact verified destination, board (existing versus suggested explicitly labeled), and suggested date.
7. Update the inventory **only using confirmed statuses**. Do not mark a pin Published from user enthusiasm or a draft posting schedule. No publishing or GitHub content change without permission.

## Editorial lessons from October 2026 Gemini experiments
- Strong: "She's Not the Family Reminder App" with ordinary counter objects; child actually sweeping for "Stop Doing the Chores They Can Learn"; sunlit Sunday tabletop; parent and child in school-morning entryway; bathroom wiping close-up for "Clean One Zone"; after-dinner cleanup for "Marathon Clean"; meal choices alongside sports commitments.
- Failed: duplicated "Give Every Paper an Exit" headline; unrelated homework still life under a chores headline. Corrected the first by keeping photo and removing the duplicate. Reject image–message mismatch instead of polishing its typography.
- Prior batch overused centered giant serif headlines above photos. Vary crop, typography, negative space, scale, and placement while maintaining brand.
- Judgments above are creative review, **not performance results**.

## Mandatory constraints
- No invented or rebuilt OMC calendar, chore chart, generator UI, or other product; use untouched approved actual assets as documented in Production.
- Existing QA prohibits **visible generated faces**. Gemini scenes with visible synthetic faces do **not** pass current publish gate, even if attractive. Prefer back views, cropped hand/object scenes, licensed real photography where permitted, or seek explicit policy change before publishing that format. Real human photographs must be properly licensed and visually truthful.
- Do not use generated writing, school forms or interfaces as supposedly accurate information. Correct garbled, repeated, missing and invented text.
- Check size/ratio and legibility from exported files; prompts alone are not proof Gemini complied.
- Avoid duplicate Pins and misleading click promises. Distinguish human approval from verified posting and actual Pinterest results.

## Success measures
Review impressions, saves, outbound clicks, outbound CTR and conversions when available, comparing topic and format. Never claim a style wins based only on a visual score.
