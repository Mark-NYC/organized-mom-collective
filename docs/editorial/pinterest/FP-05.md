- **How this differs from the Google intent:** Google results answer "how to meal plan for a family" with steps and tips. On Pinterest the observed phrasing is noun-led ("weekly meal plan family", "meal plan for a week families") and the saves are templates and menus. So pin 1 targets the noun phrase, and pin 3 is the full / short / zero framework as a page she can copy.

## Audience and intent

- **Target audience:** a busy mom who decides dinner every day at 5 pm and has tried meal plans that fell apart by midweek.
- **Search intent:** a simple, repeatable way to plan the family's dinners for the week.
- **Save intent:** she saves it for her weekend planning, or as a template to copy the full / short / zero labels onto her own week.
