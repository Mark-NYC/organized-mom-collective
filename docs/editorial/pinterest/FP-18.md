Done separately from the Google research in the brief. Record only what was observed; no invented volumes or trends.

  - Pinterest Trends: not checked (no data shown to this session).
- **How this differs from the Google intent:** Google answers "weekly vs monthly calendar" with general planning guides: a format question. On Pinterest the same need shows up as noun-led ideas ("weekly family planner", "monthly family calendar") with printable, board and meal refinements, so the pins lead with the family weekly planner idea and save the side-by-side Thursday.

## Audience and intent

- **Target audience:** Busy moms with school-age kids deciding between a monthly and a weekly wall calendar for 2027.
- **Search intent:** Decide which view keeps the family's week straight, and whether the month is enough.
