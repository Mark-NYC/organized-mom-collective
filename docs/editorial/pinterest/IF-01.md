## Audience and intent

- **Target audience:** a busy mom who wants more time for family, faith, friends and herself, but whose week is already full.
- **Search intent:** practical ways to live more intentionally as a family without adding a big new routine.
- **Save intent:** she saves it as a Sunday prompt, or for a reset at the start of a season.
