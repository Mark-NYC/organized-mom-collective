## Audience and intent

- **Target audience:** a busy mom who tried a zone or room-a-day schedule and quit because each zone took most of an evening.
- **Search intent:** a zone cleaning schedule she can actually keep on weeknights.
- **Save intent:** she saves it to set up her own zones on a weekend, or to fix a zone plan that's too big.
