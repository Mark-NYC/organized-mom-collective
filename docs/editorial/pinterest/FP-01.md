## Pinterest research

Done separately from the Google research in the brief. Only observed facts; no volumes or trend figures.

- **Primary Pinterest keyword:** weekly family schedule
- **Secondary search terms:** weekly family planner; family schedule plan; family schedule board; family organization; sunday reset
- **Researched on:** 2026-10-12
- **Evidence:**
  - Method: Pinterest's own pages, as indexed by web search restricted to pinterest.com. Pinterest search, autocomplete and Trends weren't reachable from the research environment.
  - Autocomplete for "family schedule": not checked (Pinterest unreachable). **Check in the Pinterest app before publishing pin 1.**
  - Guided search terms: not checked (same reason). Pinterest idea pages observed: "Weekly Family Planner", "Printable Weekly Family Schedule", "Create a Family Schedule Plan", "Family Schedule Board", "Family Time Schedule", "Family Day Schedule".
  - Top pin formats: editable and printable weekly family planner templates (many from Etsy), colorful chore charts, wall command centers and whiteboard schedules. What's missing: the pins sell a template; nothing observed shows the habit that keeps it current (a weekly look) or what to do when the week falls apart.
  - Pinterest Trends: not checked (unreachable).
- **How this differs from the Google intent:** Google answers "how to organize family schedule" with tips and apps. Pinterest's observed intent is a weekly page to fill in. Pin 1 targets "weekly family schedule" and promises the system; pin 3 is the Sunday reset checklist as the page to keep.

## Audience and intent

- **Target audience:** a busy mom who carries the whole week in her head and wants a simple system the family can read.
- **Search intent:** a way to organize the family's weekly schedule that keeps working after the first week.
- **Save intent:** she saves it for her Sunday planning, or as a checklist to run each weekend.
