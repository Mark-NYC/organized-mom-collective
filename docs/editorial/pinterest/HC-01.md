## Audience and intent

- **Target audience:** a mom with a job, kids and about half an hour a day, who has tried cleaning schedules before and dropped them.
- **Search intent:** a realistic day-by-day cleaning plan she can start this week, short enough to fit around work and school.
- **Save intent:** she saves it as the plan to start "next Monday", or to print for the fridge, and comes back when the house feels out of hand.
