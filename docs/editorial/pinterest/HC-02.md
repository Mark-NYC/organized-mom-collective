## Audience and intent

- **Target audience:** a busy mom whose house looks fine in the morning and wrecked by bedtime, and who wants a short nightly routine she can keep up with.
- **Search intent:** a short list of what to clean every day, in what order, and how long it takes.
- **Save intent:** she saves it to start "tonight" or to copy onto the fridge, and comes back on the evenings the kitchen gets away from her.
