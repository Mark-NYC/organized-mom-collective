## Audience and intent

- **Target audience:** a busy mom whose house slides a little every day, and who wants one short daily routine she can keep up with.
- **Search intent:** a short list of what to clean every day, in what order, and how long it takes.
- **Save intent:** she saves it to start tomorrow or to copy onto the fridge, and comes back on the days the house gets away from her.
