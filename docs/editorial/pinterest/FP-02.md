## Audience and intent

- **Target audience:** a busy mom who is the only one who knows what's happening this week and wants the family to check a calendar instead of asking her.
- **Search intent:** a simple way to set up a family calendar the whole household actually uses.
- **Save intent:** she saves it to set up or reset the family calendar on a weekend, or as a reference for the entry format and what goes on it.
