## Business and content jobs
- **Visual inspiration:** image-first galleries (pantries, mudrooms, toy storage, rooms, lighting). Earn discovery and qualified visits. Follow with a related free resource when appropriate; do not force a Club pitch onto purely decorative searches. Separate OMC-V IDs and tracker.
- **Useful/saveable content:** problem-solving mini-guides, charts, checklists, generator pins. Earn saves, tool usage and opt-in email leads; show a contextually relevant next step.
- **Seasonal pain-point/product content:** specific parenting/family-responsibility friction, actual Club kit preview, or physical calendar. Earn checkout starts and purchases. Never claim unverified features or delivery timing.
**New production hypothesis:** across the rolling last 100 new creatives, aim for 30 lifestyle gallery/inspiration, 30 editorial pain/value cards, 25 search-first guides/free tools and 15 actual Club product previews. Count by new creative, not daily quotas. Lifestyle article galleries are produced as occasional 39-photo + 10-extra-Pin sprints, typically assessed every 2–3 weeks; do not mechanically force 3 lifestyle outputs into each day. The intent is 100 Club sign-ups in the coming 12 months, measured separately from paid conversion and retention. See the daily production desk for the full workflow.\n\nThese three *content lanes* are distinct from the master plan's 6/3/1 research mix (six observed search patterns, three specific audience situations, one creative test per ten future pins). Both classifications apply to each new pin.

## Timing rules
- **Season of need** is not the first publication date.
- Plan/research/create 90–120 days ahead where possible.
- Start seasonal Pinterest publication 60–90 days before anticipated need, earlier when verified Pinterest Trends/observed search seasonality justifies it.
- Increase variations and promotion 30–60 days ahead when early evidence supports them.
- Club enrollment messaging follows the *actual* print cutoff (end of second Sunday of month) and expected delivery; do not claim a monthly kit will arrive in time without confirming fulfillment. Once cutoff passes, offer the next eligible month or email waitlist.
- Pin destinations and free-tool pages must be live and deliver the claimed benefit **before** pin publication. Evergreen pins may fill seasonal schedule gaps.
- Dates in the original 500-row master are historical **proposed** slots, not guaranteed schedule or published evidence. Never change approved IDs, overwrite approved creative, or quietly reschedule a confirmed scheduled/published pin. Proposed adjustments require a separate approval/reconciliation decision.

## Offer routing
- Inspiration gallery -> useful related article/free tool -> optional opt-in -> contextually matched Club or calendar.
- Useful pin -> matching article or generator -> immediate useful result -> email signup only when appropriate, with separate marketing consent -> specific seasonal Club offer.
- Pain/product pin -> actual monthly personalized printed kit preview or calendar page -> checkout.
- Calendar-intent articles should sell the calendar directly; Club is *not* a required gateway.
- When article topic points to a different month than current enrollment, show that kit as part of the 12-month offering but clearly explain next eligible mailing and avoid promising an unavailable print month.
- Free tool delivery must not depend on a marketing opt-in unless openly described.

## Measurement and decisions
Segment Pinterest reporting by lane, topic, season, age of pin and actual publish date. Capture impressions, saves, outbound clicks and outbound CTR per pin; on-site engaged visits, generator completions, email signups/consent, Club checkout/purchases and calendar checkout/purchases by UTM campaign/referral when feasible. Keep blank metrics blank, never substitute projections.
- 30 days: identify creative and landing-page friction; test variations, not a final Pinterest verdict.
- 60 days: compare matured cohorts by lane and destination; reallocate incremental creative toward promising combinations.
- 90 days: decide scale / refine / pause based on qualified traffic and purchase contribution, not impressions alone.
- Compare like-for-like time windows and do not infer performance from proposed publication rows or GitHub approval counts.
- Maintain a dated decision log: hypothesis, IDs, observations, decision, next test.
