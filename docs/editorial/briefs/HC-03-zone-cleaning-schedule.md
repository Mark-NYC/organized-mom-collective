## Search intent

Primary query: *zone cleaning schedule*. Intent type: how-to / template. She wants to split her house into areas and clean one a day, without a plan that eats an hour a night.

Secondary keywords (use naturally): zone cleaning, how to zone clean your house, cleaning zones, one room a day cleaning, weekly zone cleaning schedule.

Related questions (result summaries and related searches): how many zones; how to divide the house; one zone a day or one a week; how long per zone; what tasks go in each zone; printable schedule.

## What currently ranks

Research method: web search on 2026-10-12. Notes come from the search engine's summaries, not full reads of each page. Many results for the bare query are municipal street-sweeping zones; the household results:

- acultivatednest.com, "How to Do Zone Cleaning + Printable Cleaning Schedule": five zones, one per weekday, about an hour per zone with a timer, a sample plan. (https://acultivatednest.com/how-to-do-zone-cleaning-printable-cleaning-schedule/)
- FlyLady-style zone systems (printables on Etsy and Pinterest): about 15 minutes in a zone daily, a zone per week across the month.
- lifeonanewpage.com: four zones on a monthly cycle, one zone per week. (https://lifeonanewpage.com/?p=34354)
- bhg.com.au: zone cleaning explainer. (https://www.bhg.com.au/?p=1068233)
- Robot-vacuum brands (Narwal, Neato) rank for "zone cleaning" in the robot sense: a different intent.

Table stakes: divide the house; assign zones to days; a task list per zone; daily upkeep handled separately; a printable.

Gaps and weak spots:

- **Zones sized by rooms, not minutes.** Results group rooms by location and then allow an hour; nobody sizes a zone to the time she has.
- **An hour a zone** doesn't fit a weeknight; no stopping point.
- **No rule for an oversized or undersized zone** (a big kitchen, a tiny entry).
- **No fallback** when the zone day is missed, beyond "catch up".

## Differentiation

- **The Shift:** a zone isn't a room; it's 10 to 15 minutes. Size the zone to the time, not the floor plan.
- **Four steps to build your own zones**, with a sizing table (too big → split or trim; too small → combine with a neighbor), using our own week as the worked example (Entry/Bathroom is two small spots combined; Kitchen Reset gets the longest slot).
- **Four zones, not five or seven**, Monday to Thursday, weekend left open.
- **Stop when the timer goes off**; skip, don't stack.
