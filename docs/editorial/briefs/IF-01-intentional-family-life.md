Gaps and weak spots:

- **Additive.** Most advice adds activities (family nights, getaways, devotions) to a week that's already full.
- **Abstract.** "Be present", "commit to connection" with no weekly mechanism.
- **No fallback** for a hard season.
- Faith content tends to assume one tradition.

## Differentiation

- **The Shift:** being intentional isn't adding more; it's giving what matters a small, fixed spot in the week you already have, written down.
- **Six small places, two lines each:** one priority, one person, gratitude, prayer or quiet, a habit for you, shared work. Pick two to start.
- **A two-minute Sunday checklist** ("This week").
- **Respectful of every tradition:** prayer or quiet, tied to a moment that already happens. No claims about benefits of gratitude or prayer (none needed; nothing unsourced).
- **No medical or mental-health advice:** well-being stays at small habits.
