# FP-21 editorial brief: Summer Routine Chart for Kids

## Audience and promise
Busy parents of children roughly ages 3–12 who need a flexible summer day with enough structure to reduce repeated questions, without overplanning camp, play or family time.

## Primary query
summer routine chart for kids

## Related intent
summer daily schedule for kids; summer routine printable; flexible kids summer schedule; summer chores routine.

## Reader's answer
A chart of six daily anchors (morning basics, helping job, active play, quiet time, screen boundary, evening reset), with a sample weekday sequence and age adaptations.

## Distinctive value
Unlike a rigid minute-by-minute timetable, the article includes an explicit flexible day table, checkable planning steps, bad-weather and camp fallbacks, and a free editable routine chart via the existing /routine-chart tool. No standalone PDF is promised.

## Overlap boundary
FP-09 is a school-morning chart tutorial; FP-04 covers weekly planning. FP-21 is about the full summer day and flexible daytime anchors.

## Opening tension
By the second week of summer, kids need a plan, but parents do not want to direct every hour.

## The shift
A summer routine is a familiar sequence, not a fixed timetable.

## Interactive steps
Steady start, helping job, active block, quiet time, screens boundary, evening reset.

## Internal links
FP-09, FP-04, IF-03 and the free /routine-chart tool. Product CTA is the physical family wall calendar, accurately distinguished from the child's daily chart.
