# FP-22 editorial brief: Weekend routine for kids

## Audience and promise
Parents of school-age kids who want calmer weekends without planning every hour.

## Primary query
weekend routine for kids

## Related intent
weekend schedule for kids; family weekend routine; saturday routine for kids

## Reader's answer
Six anchors in the same order each weekend: breakfast together, one family job each, free play, time outside, a family meal, a Sunday-night reset. No clock times.

## Distinctive value
Timeline hero with parts of day instead of hours, weekend jobs by age, and the existing /routine-chart Weekend template (verified in src/data/routines.ts; its starter times are editable).

## Overlap boundary
