## Differentiation

- **The Shift:** the week isn't hard because there's too much on it; it's hard because it's decided one day at a time, in your head. Decide the week once, on Sunday, and write it where everyone can see.
- **Four pieces, a section each:** one calendar, one spot, a 15-minute weekly reset, dinners decided once. Each links to its deeper article (FP-02, FP-05 now; FP-03, FP-04 later).
- **A Sunday checklist** as the one screenshot-able visual.
- **A real fallback:** missed Sunday, a chaotic week, starting from scratch.
