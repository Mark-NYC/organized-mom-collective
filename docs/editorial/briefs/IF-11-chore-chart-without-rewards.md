# IF-11 editorial brief: Chore chart without rewards

## Audience and promise
Parents tired of sticker charts and "what do I get?" for everyday jobs.

## Primary query
chore chart without rewards

## Related intent
chores without rewards; kids chores no allowance; how to get kids to do chores

## Reader's answer
Everyday jobs are part of living together: three jobs tied to a moment, a chart the child checks, effort noticed out loud instead of paid for.

## Distinctive value
SayThis hero with replacement scripts, a no-rewards routine by age (jobs copied from src/data/chores-by-age.ts), and the existing free Responsibility Chart printable. Generator reward behavior verified in src/pages/chore-chart.astro: three reward boxes always render; reward text can be cleared, but the boxes cannot be switched off. The article says exactly that. No new tool built.

## Overlap boundary
IF-03 is the age-list and chart guide (its FAQ treats rewards as a family decision); IF-08 teaches one job at a time. IF-11 is only the no-rewards approach and keeps IF-03's neutral stance: rewards are optional, not wrong.

## Opening tension
The sticker chart worked two weeks; now every job starts with "What do I get?"

## The shift
The prize isn't what makes a job happen; a when, a chart and a reason do.
