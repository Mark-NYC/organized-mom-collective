# HC-10 editorial brief: Family laundry schedule

## Audience and promise
Busy moms of school-age kids whose clean laundry stalls between the dryer and the drawer.

## Primary query
laundry schedule for busy families

## Related intent
family laundry routine; laundry day schedule; kids laundry chores by age

## Reader's answer
Give each load a regular day and one owner who takes it through wash, dry, fold and put away. Two or three laundry days, chosen around the family's quiet nights.

## Distinctive value
A fill-in Builder ("On {day}, {who} {job}.") instead of a fixed universal schedule, a finish-the-load rule, and laundry jobs by age from src/data/chores-by-age.ts. Hands off to the existing free /laundry-system checklist (verified: stations, five steps, weekly schedule, PNG download).

## Overlap boundary
HC-01 covers cleaning zones, HC-02 the daily reset, IF-03 chores by age. HC-10 is only laundry and its ownership; it does not change the cleaning week in src/data/cleaning.ts.

## Opening tension
The washer ran Tuesday; Thursday the clean clothes are still on the couch.

## The shift
Most laundry piles are a putting-away problem, not a washing problem.
