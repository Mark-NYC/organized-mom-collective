## Structure

1. Tension: It’s 7:45. Someone needs a signed permission slip, the keys aren’t on the hook, and the school flyer is somewhere under yesterday’s mail. A prettier organizer won’t help if papers still stop in three different places.
2. Short answer: Put one calendar, two paper pockets and a pen where your family already passes. Give papers a way in and a way back out.
3. Shift: Give every paper an exit. A tidy inbox is insufficient without the next action.
4. Real-Life Version: choose location; calendar and pen; two labeled pockets; keys and bags nearby; after-dinner check with an agreed person.
5. Visual: five-item screenshot-ready checklist, copied exactly into the save pin.
6. Adaptations: digital calendars remain digital; no-wall setup uses two folders in a tray.
7. When Life Happens: triage tomorrow; use folders; relocate an unused station and demonstrate one form’s journey.
8. Product note: real 11 × 17 weekly calendar, verified against ARTICLE_FORMAT.md.
9. Next Small Win: label two folders and sort current school papers.
