# Search opportunity and article improvement loop

Updated: 2026-10-15. Applies to every existing and new resource article. Complements ARTICLE_WORKFLOW.md, SEO_STRATEGY.md, CONTENT_CLUSTERS.md and Pinterest production docs. Do not replace established gates.

## Weekly Search Console review (once meaningful data exists)
Export last 28 and 90 days of queries AND pages: query, landing page, impressions, clicks, CTR and average position. Compare periods, noting small samples. Console data is organic Google Search performance, **not a verified standalone AI-citation report**. Keep AI referral attribution separate in analytics.

- **Leak:** substantial impressions with relatively low CTR for the query's intent and SERP features. Inspect the actual search results, title, description and relevance; do not use universal CTR thresholds. Prioritize high-opportunity improvements with stable impressions.
- **Gap:** repeated relevant impressions for an intent without a dedicated strong landing page. Check existing article overlap before creating anything. A query at position 21 does not automatically justify a new page.
- **Near-win:** relevant query/page pair appearing near the first page, where a more precise answer, worked example, or internal links may help.
- **Protect:** pages already satisfying readers. Avoid unnecessary title and slug changes.
- Log dates, query, URL, impressions, clicks, CTR, position, intended edit, baseline and follow-up 28 days later. Do not invent search volume.
- With a new site and negligible data, research real questions and manually inspect published pages instead of manufacturing 'gaps' or 'leaks'.

## Unique usefulness requirement
For every article, identify one verifiable contribution beyond a generic AI summary: a usable printable/tool, worked household example, clear handoff of responsibility, comparison tested against one scenario, genuine first-party product photo, or specific fallback rule. Record this in the brief. Do **not** invent first-person stories, test results, testimonials, credentials, or original research. A generic anecdote is illustrative, not proof.

## Article-to-outcome flywheel
For each article: (1) distinct family search question; (2) immediate useful answer; (3) actionable routine and realistic fallback; (4) contextual guide links; (5) relevant free generator/printable where it actually exists; (6) truthful product link only when appropriate; (7) Pinterest creative production per the existing 3-pin minimum, with optional extra variations; (8) performance review. The goal is families owning more of the work, not longer pageviews.

## AI search and third-party mentions
Make pages clear, attributable, indexable, consistent in product naming and easy to quote accurately. Never guarantee AI inclusion. Earn independent brand mentions through real collaborations, guest contributions, relevant resources and honest community participation. No manufactured Reddit promotion, paid undisclosed mentions, cross-site link networks, or mass-generated near-duplicate articles.

## Monthly audit and prioritization
Audit every published article for: intent match; unique usefulness; actionable example; genuine family ownership; appropriate tool/product bridge; image relevance and honest alt; internal links; factual verification; nonduplicated keyword; current product claims; Pinterest promise/landing-page consistency; title/snippet; and performance evidence if available.

Priority: P0 broken or misleading link/claim; P1 weak search intent, overlap or missing practical answer; P2 strengthen useful example, imagery or conversion; P3 polish. Record evidence and exact action, not an unearned numeric SEO score.

## Measuring what matters
Track organic search impressions/clicks, Pinterest outbound clicks, resource-to-tool starts and completions, resource-to-calendar visits, checkout starts and purchases where tracking exists. Distinguish observed events from proposed tracking. Segment AI referrals where analytics support it, without claiming all AI-assisted discovery is attributable. Do not optimize just for article count.
