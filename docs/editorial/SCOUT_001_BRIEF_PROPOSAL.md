# Scout brief proposal: SC-001 family meeting agenda

Researched: 2026-11-03. Status: proposed for editorial approval, **not** an assigned article tracker ID or published content.

## Intent and boundaries
- Primary query: **family meeting agenda printable**
- Secondary phrases: weekly family meeting agenda; family meeting with kids; family meeting template.
- Reader wants a ready-to-use agenda for a short household conversation and a way to leave with agreed owners. Search results include fillable and downloadable one-page agendas.
- Existing FP-04 `/resources/weekly-family-reset` is specifically a 15-minute planning check: calendar, rides, dinners, extra jobs and one priority. It says kids can flag needs but need not sit through all details.
- Existing IF-02 `/resources/how-to-share-the-mental-load` addresses handing over an entire job, including noticing and reminding. Its two-week ownership trial is separate from a family meeting agenda.
- **Decision:** a dedicated family meeting *participation* agenda is distinct if it centers on hearing each person, choosing one shared decision, assigning who owns the follow-up, and closing the conversation. Do not repeat FP-04's five-step planning checklist or IF-02's complete job-handoff guide.

## Evidence
- [Alpacto family meeting agenda](https://alpacto.com/printables/family-meeting-agenda/) offers a one-page 20-minute meeting layout with changing meeting leader and note-taker.
- [Upkeep Papers family meeting agenda/actions sheet](https://upkeeppapers.com/resources/kids-and-family-life/family-meeting-agenda-and-actions-sheet/) offers a downloadable/fillable agenda.
- [Tired Mom Supermom weekly family meeting PDF](https://tiredmomsupermom.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Family_Meeting_Agenda_Template-1.pdf) includes schedule, responsibilities, gratitude and goals.
- These demonstrate format and competitive features only; they do not establish keyword search volume, ranking difficulty or OMC traffic.

## Original practical value
Create an actual **one-page family meeting agenda**, not a text-only article calling itself printable. Suggested fields:
1. One good thing from the week (everyone who wants to share)
2. One challenge we can solve together
3. One decision for this week
4. Who owns the next action and by when
5. When we'll check back

Allow a five-minute version using items 2–4. Explain that a child can contribute ideas without being made responsible for adult decisions. An illustrative filled-in example should show a real decision and an owner; label it as an example, not a customer story.

A printable PDF or equivalent accessible print view must exist and be tested *before* publishing an article that promises a printable. Do not repackage private Club welcome materials as a free download without a product decision.

- Shift: the goal isn't another meeting Mom has to manage; it is a shared conversation that ends with shared ownership.
