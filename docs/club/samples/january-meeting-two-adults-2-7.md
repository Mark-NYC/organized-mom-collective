# Our First Family Meeting · The Okafor Family

_Sample: Two adults, children 2 and 7. Generated by scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts._

Who: Ngozi (grown-up, Grown-up), Daniel (grown-up, Grown-up), Ada (2, Little one (under 3)), Tobi (7, Younger child (6–8))

About 10 minutes, together. The same four parts every month.

## Before we start
- Pick 10 minutes when everyone’s home. A meal you already share works well.
- Put this guide, a pen and your family calendar where everyone can see them.
- Hand this guide to Daniel, who opens the meeting.
- Little ones can sit on a lap. A snack helps everyone sit for ten minutes.

## 1. Our Focus · 2 min · led by Daniel
_Say why we’re meeting, in one minute._

> This is our first Family Meeting. Once a month we’ll sit down for ten minutes so everyone knows the plan and everyone has a part. Nobody has to keep it all in their head.

Why it matters: Mornings go smoother when the night before has a plan everyone knows.

- **Ngozi:** Shares in one sentence why this matters to them, without listing anyone’s misses.
- **Daniel:** Names one thing they’d love the family to share.
- **Ada:** Claps to start the meeting with everyone.
- **Tobi:** Tells one thing that would make our week easier.

## 2. Let’s Talk · 3 min · led by Daniel
_Everyone answers, youngest first. No wrong answers, no fixing anyone._

> Let’s each answer, starting with the youngest. We’re just listening today.

1. What’s happening for each of us this week, like soccer?
2. What’s one thing that would make our week feel easier?
3. What’s the trickiest part of our mornings?

- **Ada:** Points to something they like doing with the family, or says one word.
- **Tobi:** Names one thing coming up for them this week.
- **Ngozi:** Shares their own week the same way the kids do.
- **Daniel:** Listens first, and asks “What would help?” before offering a fix.

## 3. Everyone’s Part · 3 min · led by Daniel
_Each person picks one small part they own this month._

> Everyone picks one small part for this month. It’s yours, so nobody needs to remind you.

- **Ngozi:** Adds only their own plans to the calendar and leaves the kids’ plans to them. _(Finished looks like: Their own plans are on it; nobody else’s.)_
- **Daniel:** Adds their own work and plans to the calendar each week. _(Finished looks like: Their week is on the calendar.)_
- **Ada:** Joins in and watches. No job yet.
- **Tobi:** Checks the calendar each evening and lays out what they need for tomorrow. _(Finished looks like: Tomorrow’s things are out before bed.)_

## 4. Let’s Practice · 2 min · led by Tobi
_Try it right now, so the first time isn’t a reminder later._

> Let’s try it now. Everyone finds their next thing and puts it on the calendar.

- **Ngozi:** Adds their own next two weeks, and leaves the kids’ entries to the kids.
- **Daniel:** Writes next month’s meeting on the calendar.
- **Ada:** Puts a sticker on today’s date.
- **Tobi:** Writes their own next thing on the calendar. Spelling doesn’t matter.

## What we agreed
- [ ] The night before a busy day, everyone gets their own things ready. _(Bags, shoes and gear are out before bed.)_
- [ ] Take two minutes each weekend to look at the week ahead together. _(We looked at the week before it started.)_

## Our meeting rhythm
Every month: **Sunday after lunch**
- Once a month, same day and time, so nobody has to call the meeting.
- Start with one thing that went well last month.
- End on time, even if you didn’t get to everything.

Personalized from: names, ages, activities, priorities
