# Our First Family Meeting · The Ortiz Family

_Sample: Single parent with a preschooler, no profile answers. Generated by scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts._

Who: Lucia (grown-up, Grown-up), Mateo (4, Preschooler (3–5))

**Page 1: running the meeting**

### Before we start
- Pick 10 minutes when everyone’s home. A meal you already share works well.
- Put this guide, a pen and your family calendar where everyone can see them.
- You open this first meeting. As the kids grow, they can take a part.
- Little ones can sit on a lap. A snack helps everyone sit for ten minutes.

## 1. Our Focus · 2 min · what matters · led by Lucia
> This is our first Family Meeting. Once a month we’ll sit down for ten minutes so everyone knows the plan and everyone has a part. Nobody has to keep it all in their head.

What matters most right now:
- **Starting our monthly Family Meeting** _(from the month (no answers yet))_

## 2. Let’s Talk · 3 min · discuss · led by Lucia
> Let’s each answer, starting with the youngest. We’re just listening today.

1. What’s one thing happening this week that everyone should know about?
2. What would make this meeting something we all want to come to?

_Little ones answer first, by pointing or with one word._

**What gets in the way:** What usually gets in our way? Pick one thing to try changing this month. ______

**Page 2: this month’s plan**

## 3. Everyone’s Part · 3 min · decide · led by Lucia
> Here’s an idea for each of us. Keep it, swap it or make it smaller. It’s yours, so nobody needs to remind you.

- **Lucia:** Adds only their own plans to the calendar; the kids add theirs. · _When:_ As plans are made · _Done when:_ Their own plans are on it, nobody else’s.
- **Mateo:** Puts a sticker on today’s date on the calendar. · _When:_ After breakfast · _Done when:_ Today has a sticker. · _Do it together the first week, then let them lead._

## 4. Let’s Practice · 2 min · do · led by Lucia
> Let’s each try our part once, right now.

Everyone does the first step of their part once, right now. Hang it, lay it out, or write it on the calendar.

### Our meeting rhythm
Every month: **Sunday after lunch**
- Once a month, same day and time, so nobody has to call the meeting.
- Start with one thing that went well last month.
- End on time, even if you didn’t get to everything.
