# Our First Family Meeting · The Ortiz Family

_Sample: Single parent with a preschooler. Generated by scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts._

Who: Lucia (grown-up, Grown-up), Mateo (4, Preschooler (3–5))

About 10 minutes, together. The same four parts every month.

## Before we start
- Pick 10 minutes when everyone’s home. A meal you already share works well.
- Put this guide, a pen and your family calendar where everyone can see them.
- You open this first meeting. As the kids grow, they can take a part.
- Little ones can sit on a lap. A snack helps everyone sit for ten minutes.

## 1. Our Focus · 2 min · led by Lucia
_Say why we’re meeting, in one minute._

> This is our first Family Meeting. Once a month we’ll sit down for ten minutes so everyone knows the plan and everyone has a part. Nobody has to keep it all in their head.

Why it matters: When everyone can see the plan, nobody has to carry it alone.

- **Lucia:** Shares in one sentence why this matters to them, without listing anyone’s misses.
- **Mateo:** Rings a bell or claps to start the meeting.

## 2. Let’s Talk · 3 min · led by Lucia
_Everyone answers, youngest first. No wrong answers, no fixing anyone._

> Let’s each answer, starting with the youngest. We’re just listening today.

1. What’s one thing happening this week that everyone should know about?
2. What’s one thing that would make our week feel easier?

- **Mateo:** Answers first: their favorite thing we do together.
- **Lucia:** Shares their own week the same way the kids do.

## 3. Everyone’s Part · 3 min · led by Lucia
_Each person picks one small part they own this month._

> Everyone picks one small part for this month. It’s yours, so nobody needs to remind you.

- **Lucia:** Adds only their own plans to the calendar and leaves the kids’ plans to them. _(Finished looks like: Their own plans are on it; nobody else’s.)_
- **Mateo:** Puts a sticker on today’s date on the calendar each morning. _(Finished looks like: Today has a sticker.)_

## 4. Let’s Practice · 2 min · led by Lucia
_Try it right now, so the first time isn’t a reminder later._

> Let’s try it now. Everyone finds their next thing and puts it on the calendar.

- **Lucia:** Adds their own next two weeks, and leaves the kids’ entries to the kids.
- **Mateo:** Draws a small picture next to one thing that’s theirs.

## What we agreed
- [ ] Hang the family calendar where everyone walks past every day. _(Anyone can find today’s plan without asking.)_
- [ ] Everyone keeps the part they picked today for the whole month. _(At next month’s meeting, each person says how their part went.)_

## Our meeting rhythm
Every month: **Sunday after lunch**
- Once a month, same day and time, so nobody has to call the meeting.
- Start with one thing that went well last month.
- End on time, even if you didn’t get to everything.
