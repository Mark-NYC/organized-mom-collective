### 1. Our Focus
_What we’re working on together, and why it helps all of us._

- **Starting our Family Meetings** · A short, regular time when everyone hears the plan and has a say.

### 2. Let’s Talk
_Everyone answers, the youngest first. No wrong answers, no fixing anyone._

**Conversation starters**
- What’s one thing happening this week that everyone should know about?
- What would make this meeting something we all want to come to?
- What usually gets in our way? Pick one thing to try changing.

_Little ones answer first, by pointing or with one word._

---

### 3. Everyone’s Part
_Each person picks or agrees to one small part they own this month._

_Ideas to choose from, not jobs. Decide together; one small part each is plenty._

- **Mateo** · 4: Puts a sticker on today’s date on the calendar. **or** Puts their shoes by the door the night before an outing. _(Do it together at first, then let them lead.)_
- Lucia: add your own plans to the calendar, and leave the kids’ plans to them.

### 4. Let’s Practice
_Try one part together right now, so the first time isn’t a reminder later._

**Ways to make it stick**
- Try a part once, right now, while everyone’s together.
- Give each part a clear “when” that follows something already done: “After breakfast, Mateo puts a sticker on today’s date on the calendar.”
- Make it easy for Mateo: hang the calendar at their eye level.
- If a part keeps slipping, make it smaller instead of reminding more.

**Coming back to it:** At a later meeting, come back to a part: on my own, with reminders, or hard? Keep it, shrink it or swap it.
