# Our First Family Meeting · The Okafor Family

_Sample: 1 · Younger children who need a lot of support (2 and 4, two adults). Generated by scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts._

Who: Ngozi (grown-up, Grown-up), Daniel (grown-up, Grown-up), Ada (2, Little one (under 3)), Tobi (4, Preschooler (3–5))

**Page 1: running the meeting**

### Before we start
- Pick 10 minutes when everyone’s home. A meal you already share works well.
- Put this guide, a pen and your family calendar where everyone can see them.
- Hand this guide to Daniel, who opens the meeting.
- Little ones can sit on a lap. A snack helps everyone sit for ten minutes.

## 1. Our Focus · 2 min · what matters · led by Daniel
> This is our first Family Meeting. Once a month we’ll sit down for ten minutes so everyone knows the plan and everyone has a part. Nobody has to keep it all in their head.

What matters most right now:
- **Easier evenings** _(from her priority)_
- **Smoother daily routines** _(from her challenge)_

## 2. Let’s Talk · 3 min · discuss · led by Daniel
> Let’s each answer, starting with the youngest. We’re just listening today.

1. What’s one thing happening this week that everyone should know about?
2. What would make our evenings calmer?
3. Which part of our day feels most rushed?

_Little ones answer first, by pointing or with one word._

**What might get in the way:** The routine might have too many steps to own at once. **One thing to try:** Each person owns one step this month, not the whole routine. _Does that fit?_

**Page 2: this month’s plan**

## 3. Everyone’s Part · 3 min · decide · led by Daniel
> Here’s an idea for each of us. Keep it, swap it or make it smaller. It’s yours, so nobody needs to remind you.

- **Ngozi:** Adds only their own plans to the calendar; the kids add theirs. · _When:_ As plans are made · _Done when:_ Their own plans are on it, nobody else’s.
- **Daniel:** Adds their own work and plans to the calendar. · _When:_ Sunday evening · _Done when:_ Their week is on the calendar. · Already takes care of driving and pickups. Keep it up: no new job needed.
- **Ada:** Joins in and watches. No job yet.
- **Tobi:** Puts their shoes and coat in their spot. · _When:_ Coming in the door · _Done when:_ Shoes and coat in their spot. · _Do it together the first week, then let them lead._ `from her answers`

## 4. Let’s Practice · 2 min · do · led by Daniel
> Let’s each try our part once, right now.

Everyone does the first step of their part once, right now. Hang it, lay it out, or write it on the calendar.

### Before our next meeting
- [ ] For Tobi: Put a low hook and a shoe spot by the door.
- [ ] Hang the family calendar where everyone walks past every day.

_Next meeting, each person says how their part went: on my own, with reminders, or hard._

### Our meeting rhythm
Every month: **Sunday after lunch**
- Once a month, same day and time, so nobody has to call the meeting.
- Start with one thing that went well last month.
- End on time, even if you didn’t get to everything.
