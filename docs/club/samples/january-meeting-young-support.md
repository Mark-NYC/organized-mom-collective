### 1. Our Focus
_What we’re working on together, and why it helps all of us._

- **Easier evenings** · Each person owns one small piece of the evening.
- **Smoother daily routines** · Routines the kids know well enough to start on their own.

### 2. Let’s Talk
_Everyone answers, the youngest first. No wrong answers, no fixing anyone._

**Conversation starters**
- What’s one thing happening this week that everyone should know about?
- What would make our evenings calmer?
- Which part of our day feels most rushed?
- What gets in our way? One possibility: the routine might have too many steps to own at once.

_Little ones answer first, by pointing or with one word._

---

### 3. Everyone’s Part
_Each person picks or agrees to one small part they own this month._

_Ideas to choose from, not jobs. Decide together; one small part each is plenty._

- **Ada** · 2: joins in and watches.
- **Tobi** · 4: Puts their shoes and coat in their spot. **or** Puts a sticker on today’s date on the calendar. _(Do it together at first, then let them lead.)_
- Ngozi and Daniel: add your own plans to the calendar, and leave the kids’ plans to them.
- Daniel already takes care of driving and pickups. Notice it out loud.

### 4. Let’s Practice
_Try one part together right now, so the first time isn’t a reminder later._

**Ways to make it stick**
- Try a part once, right now, while everyone’s together.
- Each person owns one step this month, not the whole routine.
- Give each part a clear “when” that follows something already done: “Coming in the door, Tobi puts their shoes and coat in their spot.”
- Make it easy for Tobi: put a low hook and a shoe spot by the door.

**Coming back to it:** At a later meeting, come back to a part: on my own, with reminders, or hard? Keep it, shrink it or swap it.
