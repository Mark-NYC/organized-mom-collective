# Our Family Meeting Companion
**January 2027 · The Okafor Family**

> Sample: 1 · Younger children who need a lot of support (2 and 4, two adults). Generated by `scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts`.

Ideas for our January Family Meetings.

_Use the four parts from your Family Meeting guide. Pick what fits at each meeting, come back to what matters, skip the rest._

### 1. Our Focus
_What we’re working on together, and why it helps all of us._

- **Easier evenings** · Each person owns one small piece of the evening.
- **Smoother daily routines** · Routines the kids know well enough to start on their own.

### 2. Let’s Talk
_Everyone answers, the youngest first. No wrong answers, no fixing anyone._

**Conversation starters**
- What’s one thing happening this week that everyone should know about?
- What would make our evenings calmer?
- Which part of our day feels most rushed?
- What gets in our way? One possibility: the routine might have too many steps to own at once.

_Little ones can point or answer with one word._

---

### 3. Everyone’s Part
_Each person picks or agrees to one small part they own this month._

_Ideas to choose from, not jobs. Decide together._

- **Ada** · 2: joins in and watches.
- **Tobi** · 4: Coat on the hook, shoes in the shoe spot. **or** Put a sticker on today’s date on the calendar. _(Do it together at first, then step back.)_
- For the grown-ups to talk through: how will we help without taking over, and how will we notice the effort?
- Daniel already takes care of driving and pickups. Worth noticing out loud.

### 4. Let’s Practice
_Try one part together right now, so the first time isn’t a reminder later._

**Ways to make it stick**
- If there’s time, try a part together before the meeting ends.
- One idea: each person takes one step of the routine instead of the whole thing.
- It can help to tie a part to something that already happens. For Tobi: “Coming in the door, coat on the hook, shoes in the shoe spot.”
- To make it easier for Tobi: a low hook and a shoe spot by the door.

**Coming back to it:** At a later meeting, you might check how a part is going: on my own, with reminders, or hard. Keep it, shrink it or swap it.
