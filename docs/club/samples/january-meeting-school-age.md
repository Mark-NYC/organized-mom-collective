# Our First Family Meeting · The Park Family

_Sample: 2 · School-aged children building independence (7 and 10, one adult). Generated by scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts._

Who: Hana (grown-up, Grown-up), Jun (7, Younger child (6–8)), Mira (10, Older child (9–12))

**Page 1: running the meeting**

### Before we start
- Pick 10 minutes when everyone’s home. A meal you already share works well.
- Put this guide, a pen and your family calendar where everyone can see them.
- Hand this guide to Mira, who opens the meeting.

## 1. Our Focus · 2 min · what matters · led by Mira
> This is our first Family Meeting. Once a month we’ll sit down for ten minutes so everyone knows the plan and everyone has a part. Nobody has to keep it all in their head.

What matters most right now:
- **School things ready on time** _(from her priority)_
- **Knowing who does what** _(from her challenge)_

## 2. Let’s Talk · 3 min · discuss · led by Mira
> Let’s each answer, starting with the youngest. We’re just listening today.

1. What’s one thing happening this week that everyone should know about?
2. What do we usually forget for school?
3. Who does what at our house right now?

**What might get in the way:** Jobs might not have a clear “when”. **One thing to try:** Give each person’s part a “when” that follows something they already do. _Does that fit?_

**Page 2: this month’s plan**

## 3. Everyone’s Part · 3 min · decide · led by Mira
> Here’s an idea for each of us. Keep it, swap it or make it smaller. It’s yours, so nobody needs to remind you.

- **Hana:** Adds only their own plans to the calendar; the kids add theirs. · _When:_ As plans are made · _Done when:_ Their own plans are on it, nobody else’s.
- **Jun:** Packs their own bag for school the night before. · _When:_ After dinner · _Done when:_ Bag packed and on the hook before bed. · _Show it once; one reminder the first week, then step back._ `from her answers`
- **Mira:** Starts homework on their own and asks for help when stuck. · _When:_ After their snack · _Done when:_ Started on their own; asked if stuck. · _On their own. Check in at the next meeting._ · Already takes care of pet care. Keep it up: no new job needed. `from her answers`

## 4. Let’s Practice · 2 min · do · led by Jun
> Let’s each try our part once, right now.

Everyone does the first step of their part once, right now. Hang it, lay it out, or write it on the calendar.

### Before our next meeting
- [ ] For Jun: Put a hook by the door at their height.
- [ ] For Mira: Keep supplies at the homework spot.

_Next meeting, each person says how their part went: on my own, with reminders, or hard._

### Our meeting rhythm
Every month: **Sunday after lunch**
- Once a month, same day and time, so nobody has to call the meeting.
- Start with one thing that went well last month.
- End on time, even if you didn’t get to everything.
