# Our Family Meeting Companion
**January 2027 · The Park Family**

> Sample: 2 · School-aged children building independence (7 and 10, one adult). Generated by `scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts`.

Ideas for our January Family Meetings.

_Use the four parts from your Family Meeting guide. Pick what fits at each meeting, come back to what matters, skip the rest._

### 1. Our Focus
_What we’re working on together, and why it helps all of us._

- **School things ready on time** · Kids get their own school things ready the night before.
- **Knowing who does what** · Everyone knows their own part without being told.

### 2. Let’s Talk
_Everyone answers, the youngest first. No wrong answers, no fixing anyone._

**Conversation starters**
- What’s one thing happening this week that everyone should know about?
- What do we usually forget for school?
- Who does what at our house right now?
- What gets in our way? One possibility: jobs might not have a clear “when”.

---

### 3. Everyone’s Part
_Each person picks or agrees to one small part they own this month._

_Ideas to choose from, not jobs. Decide together._

- **Jun** · 7: Pack the school bag the night before. **or** Check tomorrow on the calendar and lay out what’s needed. _(Show it once, then step back.)_
- **Mira** · 10: Start homework solo, and ask for help when stuck. **or** Add new activities to the family calendar. _(Ready for more independence.)_ · Mira already takes care of pet care. Worth noticing out loud.
- For the grown-ups to talk through: how will we help without taking over, and how will we notice the effort?

### 4. Let’s Practice
_Try one part together right now, so the first time isn’t a reminder later._

**Ways to make it stick**
- If there’s time, try a part together before the meeting ends.
- One idea: tie each part to something that already happens, like after dinner or coming in the door.
- To make it easier for Jun: a hook by the door at the right height.
- To make it easier for Mira: supplies kept at the homework spot.

**Coming back to it:** At a later meeting, you might check how a part is going: on my own, with reminders, or hard. Keep it, shrink it or swap it.
