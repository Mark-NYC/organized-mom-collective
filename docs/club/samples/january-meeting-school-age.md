### 1. Our Focus
_What we’re working on together, and why it helps all of us._

- **School things ready on time** · Kids get their own school things ready the night before.
- **Knowing who does what** · Everyone knows their own part without being told.

### 2. Let’s Talk
_Everyone answers, the youngest first. No wrong answers, no fixing anyone._

**Conversation starters**
- What’s one thing happening this week that everyone should know about?
- What do we usually forget for school?
- Who does what at our house right now?
- What gets in our way? One possibility: jobs might not have a clear “when”.

---

### 3. Everyone’s Part
_Each person picks or agrees to one small part they own this month._

_Ideas to choose from, not jobs. Decide together; one small part each is plenty._

- **Jun** · 7: Packs their own bag for school the night before. **or** Checks tomorrow on the calendar and lays out what they need. _(Show it once, then step back.)_
- **Mira** · 10: Starts homework on their own and asks for help when stuck. **or** Adds their own activities to the family calendar. _(Can do it on their own.)_ · Mira already takes care of pet care. Notice it out loud.
- Hana: add your own plans to the calendar, and leave the kids’ plans to them.

### 4. Let’s Practice
_Try one part together right now, so the first time isn’t a reminder later._

**Ways to make it stick**
- Try a part once, right now, while everyone’s together.
- Give each person’s part a “when” that follows something they already do.
- Make it easy for Jun: put a hook by the door at their height.
- Make it easy for Mira: keep supplies at the homework spot.

**Coming back to it:** At a later meeting, come back to a part: on my own, with reminders, or hard? Keep it, shrink it or swap it.
