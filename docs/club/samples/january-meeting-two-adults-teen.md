# Our First Family Meeting · The Lindqvist Family

_Sample: Two adults with a teenager. Generated by scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts._

Who: Maja (grown-up, Grown-up), Sam (grown-up, Grown-up), Elsa (15, Teen (13 and up))

About 10 minutes, together. The same four parts every month.

## Before we start
- Pick 10 minutes when everyone’s home. A meal you already share works well.
- Put this guide, a pen and your family calendar where everyone can see them.
- Hand this guide to Sam, who opens the meeting.

## 1. Our Focus · 2 min · led by Sam
_Say why we’re meeting, in one minute._

> This is our first Family Meeting. Once a month we’ll sit down for ten minutes so everyone knows the plan and everyone has a part. Nobody has to keep it all in their head.

Why it matters: You want to share keeping track of activities and rides. It starts with everyone seeing the plan.

- **Maja:** Shares in one sentence why this matters to them, without listing anyone’s misses.
- **Sam:** Names one thing they’d love the family to share.
- **Elsa:** Gives one real example from our week where a heads-up would have helped.

## 2. Let’s Talk · 3 min · led by Elsa
_Everyone answers, youngest first. No wrong answers, no fixing anyone._

> Let’s each answer, starting with the youngest. We’re just listening today.

1. What’s happening for each of us this week, like robotics?
2. What’s one thing that would make our week feel easier?
3. Which ride or activity catches us by surprise most often?

- **Elsa:** Walks through their own week: plans, practices, anything that needs a ride or money.
- **Maja:** Shares their own week the same way the kids do.
- **Sam:** Listens first, and asks “What would help?” before offering a fix.

## 3. Everyone’s Part · 3 min · led by Sam
_Each person picks one small part they own this month._

> Everyone picks one small part for this month. It’s yours, so nobody needs to remind you.

- **Maja:** Adds only their own plans to the calendar and leaves the kids’ plans to them. _(Finished looks like: Their own plans are on it; nobody else’s.)_
- **Sam:** Adds their own work and plans to the calendar each week. _(Finished looks like: Their week is on the calendar.)_
- **Elsa:** Keeps their own plans on the family calendar, including rides they need. _(Finished looks like: Their month is on the calendar, rides included.)_

## 4. Let’s Practice · 2 min · led by Elsa
_Try it right now, so the first time isn’t a reminder later._

> Let’s try it now. Everyone finds their next thing and puts it on the calendar.

- **Maja:** Adds their own next two weeks, and leaves the kids’ entries to the kids.
- **Sam:** Writes next month’s meeting on the calendar.
- **Elsa:** Adds their next two weeks and checks for clashes with anyone else’s.

## What we agreed
- [ ] Everyone 9 and up adds their own plans to the calendar. _(Nobody adds plans for anyone 9 and up.)_
- [ ] Take two minutes each weekend to look at the week ahead together. _(We looked at the week before it started.)_

## Our meeting rhythm
Every month: **Sunday after lunch**
- Once a month, same day and time, so nobody has to call the meeting.
- Start with one thing that went well last month.
- End on time, even if you didn’t get to everything.

Personalized from: names, ages, activities, priorities
