# Our First Family Meeting · The Lindqvist Family

_Sample: Two adults with a teenager. Generated by scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts._

Who: Maja (grown-up, Grown-up), Sam (grown-up, Grown-up), Elsa (15, Teen (13 and up))

**Page 1: running the meeting**

### Before we start
- Pick 10 minutes when everyone’s home. A meal you already share works well.
- Put this guide, a pen and your family calendar where everyone can see them.
- Hand this guide to Sam, who opens the meeting.

## 1. Our Focus · 2 min · what matters · led by Sam
> This is our first Family Meeting. Once a month we’ll sit down for ten minutes so everyone knows the plan and everyone has a part. Nobody has to keep it all in their head.

What matters most right now:
- **Fewer reminders** _(from her priority)_
- **Sharing plans with each other** _(from her challenge)_

## 2. Let’s Talk · 3 min · discuss · led by Elsa
> Let’s each answer, starting with the youngest. We’re just listening today.

1. What’s happening for each of us this week, like robotics?
2. What could each of us remember on our own if we knew when to do it?
3. How does each of us like to hear about plans?

**What might get in the way:** Plans might be getting shared in passing. **One thing to try:** New plans get said out loud at the meeting and written on the calendar that day. _Does that fit?_

**Page 2: this month’s plan**

## 3. Everyone’s Part · 3 min · decide · led by Sam
> Here’s an idea for each of us. Keep it, swap it or make it smaller. It’s yours, so nobody needs to remind you.

- **Maja:** Adds only their own plans to the calendar; the kids add theirs. · _When:_ As plans are made · _Done when:_ Their own plans are on it, nobody else’s.
- **Sam:** Adds their own work and plans to the calendar. · _When:_ Sunday evening · _Done when:_ Their week is on the calendar.
- **Elsa:** Sets their own wind-down time on school nights and keeps it. · _When:_ Their own phone alarm · _Done when:_ Keeps their bedtime most school nights. · _Their way. Check in at the next meeting._ `from her answers`

## 4. Let’s Practice · 2 min · do · led by Elsa
> Let’s each try our part once, right now.

Everyone does the first step of their part once, right now. Hang it, lay it out, or write it on the calendar.

### Before our next meeting
- [ ] Hang the family calendar where everyone walks past every day.
- [ ] Write next month’s meeting on the calendar.

_Next meeting, each person says how their part went: on my own, with reminders, or hard._

### Our meeting rhythm
Every month: **Sunday after lunch**
- Once a month, same day and time, so nobody has to call the meeting.
- Start with one thing that went well last month.
- End on time, even if you didn’t get to everything.

Personalized from: names, ages, priorities, activities, growth areas
