# Our First Family Meeting · The Bennett Family

_Sample: Single parent, children 5, 9 and 13. Generated by scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts._

Who: Kara (grown-up, Grown-up), Mia (5, Preschooler (3–5)), Leo (9, Older child (9–12)), Jade (13, Teen (13 and up))

About 10 minutes, together. The same four parts every month.

## Before we start
- Pick 10 minutes when everyone’s home. A meal you already share works well.
- Put this guide, a pen and your family calendar where everyone can see them.
- Hand this guide to Jade, who opens the meeting.
- Little ones can sit on a lap. A snack helps everyone sit for ten minutes.

## 1. Our Focus · 2 min · led by Jade
_Say why we’re meeting, in one minute._

> This is our first Family Meeting. Once a month we’ll sit down for ten minutes so everyone knows the plan and everyone has a part. Nobody has to keep it all in their head.

Why it matters: You want to stop being everyone’s reminder. When everyone sees the plan, they can remember their own part.

- **Kara:** Shares in one sentence why this matters to them, without listing anyone’s misses.
- **Mia:** Rings a bell or claps to start the meeting.
- **Leo:** Reads the focus out loud.
- **Jade:** Gives one real example from our week where a heads-up would have helped.

## 2. Let’s Talk · 3 min · led by Leo
_Everyone answers, youngest first. No wrong answers, no fixing anyone._

> Let’s each answer, starting with the youngest. We’re just listening today.

1. What’s happening for each of us this week, like piano?
2. What’s one thing that would make our week feel easier?
3. What’s something you could remember on your own if it were on the calendar?

- **Mia:** Answers first: their favorite thing we do together.
- **Leo:** Names what’s on for them this week and what they’ll need for it.
- **Jade:** Walks through their own week: plans, practices, anything that needs a ride or money.
- **Kara:** Shares their own week the same way the kids do.

## 3. Everyone’s Part · 3 min · led by Jade
_Each person picks one small part they own this month._

> Everyone picks one small part for this month. It’s yours, so nobody needs to remind you.

- **Kara:** Adds only their own plans to the calendar and leaves the kids’ plans to them. _(Finished looks like: Their own plans are on it; nobody else’s.)_
- **Mia:** Puts a sticker on today’s date on the calendar each morning. _(Finished looks like: Today has a sticker.)_
- **Leo:** Adds their own activities to the family calendar. _(Finished looks like: Everything they have this month is on the calendar.)_
- **Jade:** Keeps their own plans on the family calendar, including rides they need. _(Finished looks like: Their month is on the calendar, rides included.)_

## 4. Let’s Practice · 2 min · led by Leo
_Try it right now, so the first time isn’t a reminder later._

> Let’s try it now. Everyone finds their next thing and puts it on the calendar.

- **Kara:** Adds their own next two weeks, and leaves the kids’ entries to the kids.
- **Mia:** Draws a small picture next to one thing that’s theirs.
- **Leo:** Writes in their own plans for the next two weeks.
- **Jade:** Adds their next two weeks and checks for clashes with anyone else’s.

## What we agreed
- [ ] Everyone 9 and up adds their own plans to the calendar. _(Nobody adds plans for anyone 9 and up.)_
- [ ] Check the calendar first before asking “What are we doing?” _(Fewer “What’s happening?” questions.)_

## Our meeting rhythm
Every month: **Sunday after lunch**
- Once a month, same day and time, so nobody has to call the meeting.
- Start with one thing that went well last month.
- End on time, even if you didn’t get to everything.

Personalized from: names, ages, activities, priorities
