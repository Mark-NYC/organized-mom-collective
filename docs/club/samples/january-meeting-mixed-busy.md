Ideas for this month’s meetings, made for our family. Use what fits, come back to what matters, skip the rest.

### 1. Our Focus
_What we’re working on together, and why it helps all of us._

- **Sharing activities and rides** · Everyone sees what’s coming, and the kids help get ready for their own activities.
- **Sharing the work** · The work at home is spread so no one carries most of it.

### 2. Let’s Talk
_Everyone answers, the youngest first. No wrong answers, no fixing anyone._

**Conversation starters**
- What’s happening for each of us this week, like piano?
- Which activity or ride catches us by surprise most?
- Which jobs at home could someone else try?
- What gets in our way? One possibility: plans might be living in one person’s head or phone.

_Little ones answer first, by pointing or with one word._

### 3. Everyone’s Part
_Each person picks or agrees to one small part they own this month._

_Ideas to choose from, not jobs. Decide together; one small part each is plenty._

- **Mia** · 5: Puts their shoes and coat in their spot. **or** Sets out the napkins and spoons. _(Do it together at first, then let them lead.)_
- **Leo** · 9: Clears and rinses the dishes after dinner. **or** Keeps track of their own gear and water bottle. _(Can do it on their own.)_
- **Jade** · 13: Keeps track of their own things, keys and chargers included. **or** Helps cook one dinner a week. _(Their way, their call.)_ · Jade already takes care of laundry. Notice it out loud.
- Kara and Marcus: add your own plans to the calendar, and leave the kids’ plans to them.
- Marcus already takes care of driving and pickups. Notice it out loud.

### 4. Let’s Practice
_Try one part together right now, so the first time isn’t a reminder later._

**Ways to make it stick**
- Try a part once, right now, while everyone’s together.
- Hang the calendar where everyone passes, and add plans the day they’re made.
- Give each part a clear “when” that follows something already done: “Coming in the door, Mia puts their shoes and coat in their spot.”
- Make it easy for Mia: put a low hook and a shoe spot by the door.

**Coming back to it:** At a later meeting, come back to a part: on my own, with reminders, or hard? Keep it, shrink it or swap it.
