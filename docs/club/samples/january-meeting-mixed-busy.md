# Our First Family Meeting · The Bennett Family

_Sample: 3 · Children of different ages with competing schedules (5, 9 and 13, two adults). Generated by scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts._

Who: Kara (grown-up, Grown-up), Marcus (grown-up, Grown-up), Mia (5, Preschooler (3–5)), Leo (9, Older child (9–12)), Jade (13, Teen (13 and up))

**Page 1: running the meeting**

### Before we start
- Pick 10 minutes when everyone’s home. A meal you already share works well.
- Put this guide, a pen and your family calendar where everyone can see them.
- Hand this guide to Marcus, who opens the meeting.
- Little ones can sit on a lap. A snack helps everyone sit for ten minutes.

## 1. Our Focus · 2 min · what matters · led by Marcus
> This is our first Family Meeting. Once a month we’ll sit down for ten minutes so everyone knows the plan and everyone has a part. Nobody has to keep it all in their head.

What matters most right now:
- **Sharing activities and rides** _(from her priority)_
- **Sharing the work** _(from her challenge)_

## 2. Let’s Talk · 3 min · discuss · led by Jade
> Let’s each answer, starting with the youngest. We’re just listening today.

1. What’s happening for each of us this week, like piano?
2. Which activity or ride catches us by surprise most?
3. Which jobs at home could someone else try?

_Little ones answer first, by pointing or with one word._

**What might get in the way:** Plans might be living in one person’s head or phone. **One thing to try:** Hang the calendar where everyone passes, and add plans the day they’re made. _Does that fit?_

**Page 2: this month’s plan**

## 3. Everyone’s Part · 3 min · decide · led by Leo
> Here’s an idea for each of us. Keep it, swap it or make it smaller. It’s yours, so nobody needs to remind you.

- **Kara:** Adds only their own plans to the calendar; the kids add theirs. · _When:_ As plans are made · _Done when:_ Their own plans are on it, nobody else’s.
- **Marcus:** Adds their own work and plans to the calendar. · _When:_ Sunday evening · _Done when:_ Their week is on the calendar. · Already takes care of driving and pickups. Keep it up: no new job needed.
- **Mia:** Puts their shoes and coat in their spot. · _When:_ Coming in the door · _Done when:_ Shoes and coat in their spot. · _Do it together the first week, then let them lead._ `from her answers`
- **Leo:** Clears and rinses the dishes after dinner. · _When:_ After dinner · _Done when:_ Dishes rinsed and stacked. · _On their own. Check in at the next meeting._ `from her answers`
- **Jade:** Keeps track of their own things, keys and chargers included. · _When:_ Coming home · _Done when:_ No searching for their things this week. · _Their way. Check in at the next meeting._ · Already takes care of laundry. Keep it up: no new job needed. `from her answers`

## 4. Let’s Practice · 2 min · do · led by Marcus
> Let’s each try our part once, right now.

Everyone does the first step of their part once, right now. Hang it, lay it out, or write it on the calendar.

### Before our next meeting
- [ ] For Mia: Put a low hook and a shoe spot by the door.
- [ ] Hang the family calendar where everyone walks past every day.

_Next meeting, each person says how their part went: on my own, with reminders, or hard._

### Our meeting rhythm
Every month: **Sunday after lunch**
- Once a month, same day and time, so nobody has to call the meeting.
- Start with one thing that went well last month.
- End on time, even if you didn’t get to everything.

Personalized from: names, ages, priorities, activities, growth areas, existing responsibilities
