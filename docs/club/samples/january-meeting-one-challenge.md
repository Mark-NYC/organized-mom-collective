# Our Family Meeting Companion
**January 2027 · The Kim Family**

> Sample: 10 · Three children, one shared challenge and nothing else (4, 7 and 10, one adult). Generated by `scripts/meeting-guide-samples.ts`.

Ideas for our January Family Meetings.

_Use the four parts from your Family Meeting guide. Pick what fits at each meeting, come back to what matters, skip the rest._

### 1. Our Focus
_What we’re working on together, and why it helps all of us._

- **Fewer reminders** · The kids know when their part happens, so one person isn’t everyone’s reminder.

### 2. Let’s Talk
_Everyone answers, the youngest first. No wrong answers, no fixing anyone._

**Conversation starters**
- What do we remind each other about most?
- What gets in the way? One possibility: jobs might not have a clear “when”.

_Little ones can point or answer with one word._

---

### 3. Everyone’s Part
_Each person picks or agrees to one small part they own this month._

_Ideas to choose from, not jobs. Decide together._

- Ask Min, Joon and Seo which job they hear the most reminders about, then give that job a clear “when” together.
- For the grown-ups to talk through: how will we help without taking over, and how will we notice the effort?

### 4. Let’s Practice
_Try one part together right now, so the first time isn’t a reminder later._

**Ways to make it stick**
- One thing worth trying: tie each part to something that already happens, like after dinner or coming in the door.
- Before reminding, try asking “What’s next?” and let the answer come from the kids.

**Coming back to it:** Try it until the next meeting, then ask: on my own, with reminders, or hard? Keep it, shrink it or swap it.
