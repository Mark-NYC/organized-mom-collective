## Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [date]*

Organized Mom Collective ("we") makes a printed family wall calendar and free tools for families. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

### What we collect

- **When you buy from our website:** your name, email, shipping address and phone (for physical orders), and what you bought. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
- **When you create an account:** your email address. We email you a one-time code to sign in; there's no password.
- **What you save to your account:** the things you make with our free tools and articles, such as chore charts, weekly plans, routine charts and lists. These can include whatever you type, including your children's first names and their chores. We store them only so you can open them on your other devices.
- **When you download a free printable** and we ask for your email: your email and which tool you used.
- **If you contact support:** your message and the details you include.
- **Usage data:** pages visited and buttons used, through Google Analytics. We don't send what you type into tools to analytics.

Without an account, what you make with our tools stays in your own browser and never reaches us.

### How we use it

To fulfil orders and send order emails; to sign you in and keep your saved things in sync; to answer support requests; to improve the site. **Marketing emails only if you opt in**, separately from signing in, and you can unsubscribe anytime from any email or your account page.

### Who we share it with

Only the services that run the site: Supabase (database and sign-in), Stripe (payments), Resend (email), Vercel (hosting), Google Analytics (usage), and shipping carriers for physical orders. We don't sell your information.

### Children

Our site is for parents. We don't knowingly collect information from children. Names or chores a parent types into a tool are stored only for that parent's account.

### Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account anytime at [organizedmomcollective.com/account](/account): your saved things and sign-in are removed and marketing emails stop. We keep order records as required for tax and accounting. To ask for a copy or deletion of anything else, email contact@organizedmomcollective.com.

### Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to opt out of marketing. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.
