# Customer Story 002: Raquel, Worth Waiting For

**Recorded:** November 9, 2026  
**Source:** Personal WhatsApp conversation (October 16–27, 2026) and a five-star Etsy review provided by the founder  
**Product:** 2027 Family Wall Calendar, January–June edition  
**Status:** Purchase and review confirmed by supplied screenshots  
**Visibility:** Internal research. Do not publish customer messages, screenshots, or identifying details without consent.

## Story

Raquel had previously seen an early version of the family's calendar system. On October 16, she heard that the printed 2027 calendar was available and was given the Etsy listing. She preferred this calendar to other options she was considering:

> “No I liked yours so much I decided to wait.”

She followed up: “I'm about to order it.”

On October 20, after receiving shipping information, she asked whether calendar pages were available for the rest of 2026. She specifically wanted printable pages for November and December so she could begin before January. She was offered a printable weekly-planner alternative, which she welcomed:

> “Awesome! Thanks for sending that. I can definitely use something like this til next year.”

On October 27, the seller thanked her for her Etsy review.

## Etsy review: 5 stars

The following is a verbatim transcription of the review screenshot provided November 9, 2026:

> This calendar is life changing. Quite literally! When they say, it will lighten your mental load, they are not kidding.
>
> We live busy lives and often host people and have lots of activities so being able to keep up with all the activities as well as with cleaning and meals is a challenge especially while trying to eat healthy. This calendar has made such a difference. I cannot say it enough.
>
> If you're thinking of a tool to make your life and home more clean, get this immediately.

The shop's public reply thanked the reviewer for sharing how the calendar was helping her family.

## Insights

- **Preferred design:** She chose this calendar over alternatives and was willing to wait.
- **Start-now demand:** She requested November and December 2026 pages. Another early customer, Kevin, independently requested the same.
- **Customer language:** “Lighten your mental load” and “life changing” are especially compelling descriptions, grounded in an actual review.
- **Jobs to be done:** Coordinate activities, cleaning, meals, healthy eating, and hosting in a busy home.
- **Format:** Printable bridge pages can help customers begin using the system before dated physical editions start.

## Possible follow-up
