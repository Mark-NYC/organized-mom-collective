## Creative and publishing rules

- 1000 × 1500 px, Georgia for headlines and Montserrat for supporting text, footer ORGANIZED MOM COLLECTIVE.
- Brand: charcoal #545454, ivory #FBF7F1, frost blue #CDDDE1, leaf green #D4DFC4, apricot #F1D8A8.
- Mix search-led lists, routines, real printable previews, ownership teaching and personalized-generator offers. No generic identical templates, stock watermarks, brand logos on household props or faces above shoulders.
- Use the existing higher-quality responsibility-card illustrations without cropping; do not redraw or invent charts when the exact resource can be shown.
- Schedule 5 pins per day for 20 days, **only after** artwork and URLs are ready; do not collide with existing Pinterest scheduling or exceed native 100 scheduled limit. Preserve scheduled pin records and dedupe before export.
- Pins for individual ages should show real age-appropriate chores; reward systems are optional, not the headline promise. Main message: kids own jobs, not mom's reminders.
- Each pin needs a distinct visual design and specific promise; test angles rather than duplicating the same pin 100 times.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the live 4–5, 6–7, 8–9 and 10–12 guide pages and swap destinations where better than the general chart generator.
2. Produce pin images with the CSV filename field, using OMC supplied assets; inspect legibility and illustration clipping.
3. Mark READY after image QA, verify destination links, write accessible alt text and check duplicates.
4. Assign dates based on the existing Pinterest calendar, then export a Pinterest-compatible upload CSV. Keep this planning CSV separate from the final uploader format.
5. Log publish date, impressions, outbound clicks and chart email submissions by pin ID for iteration.
