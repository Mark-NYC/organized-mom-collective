# Public visual design

Owner-approved direction, October 13, 2026. The homepage inset gray hero is the reference.

- Use square, inset compositions with white breathing room around them. Shared marketing panels have 16px side gutters on phones and 36px on desktop, capped at 1504px.
- Pale gray (#f1f2f2) grounds introductory copy. Muted sage (#e7ece5) supports product and closing callouts. Keep charcoal primary buttons, the existing brand typography, and readable white article bodies.
- Bring photography and copy into one composition. Lifestyle photos can fade into a matching copy background when their subjects and useful details remain visible. Product artwork, printable instructions, and screenshots stay clear and unfaded.
- Editorial and product photos have square edges. Featured article teasers join the photo and text on a gray surface; ordinary article cards stay open and simple.
- Do not turn every section into a card or repeat a photo fade everywhere. Use white space, rules, and headings for simpler sections.
- Tool introductions use the shared visual frame. Tool controls, editable sheets, saved content, and exported printable designs remain governed by TOOL-OUTPUT-DESIGN.md.
- Public styles live in src/styles/public-design.css and load through PublicLayout. The cleaning companion uses AppLayout and retains its own interface.
- Check phone, tablet, and desktop widths after visual changes, including long titles, portrait printable examples, and download controls.
