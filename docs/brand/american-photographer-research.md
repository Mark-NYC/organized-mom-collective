## Casting + visual gate
Prioritize white family representation as requested while testing for relaxed American household context and interiors. Do not assert ethnicity solely from site tags. Reject staged portrait sessions, extreme cool grading, luxury homes, and repetitive photos that cannot fill different page functions. Select only individually verified real photos with adequate rights for proposed usage.

## Honest feasibility checkpoint
**None of these profiles alone currently proves a deep enough free, coherent set of 25 domestic scenes.** Before expanding to 100+ assets, assemble **6–10 distinct linked photo pages per top three**, across multiple subjects, and compare a contact sheet. If quality is poor, change strategy to photo-shoot-specific sourcing rather than insisting on a single photographer. Avoid claiming photos are available without confirming free (not Unsplash+) download.
