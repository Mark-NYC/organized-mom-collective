## Next production step

1. Gather 8–12 candidate images **per photographer**, from multiple scenes not just near-duplicates. Confirm creator attribution.
2. Make **photographer-specific contact sheets** before selecting any images.
3. Pick one lead photographer and at most two supporting photographers after visual assessment.
4. Validate each image's source, genuine photographic provenance, licensing, any applicable model-release/likeness permissions, and allowed contexts. No publishing without passing rights gate.
5. Build a restrained house-grade: warm-neutral white balance, soft highlight curve, lifted darks, moderate saturation; correct images individually rather than applying identical blindly.
6. Store originals outside the public site, publish optimized WebP only after review.
