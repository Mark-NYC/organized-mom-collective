# Archived: 3-Zone Fall Cleaning Map (freemium companion)

Status: **Retired model.** Owner decision, October 13, 2026. Kept for reference. The freemium approach (free starter, paid "VIP" upgrade inside the companion) is not coming back: the companion stays a full, ungated bonus for calendar owners, and the companion never carries sales elements (CLAUDE.md). The seasonal checklist idea itself lives on as a free Pinterest offer: see [PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md → Phase 1](../PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md#phase-1-brand-discovery-and-owned-audience-next-growth-work).

Source: the previous iteration's WordPress members area, fall 2025, sitting in the cleaning companion. A spring version gated the same way. Screenshot: [fall-cleaning-map-freemium.webp](fall-cleaning-map-freemium.webp).

## How it worked

- Members area nav: Home · Tidy · month list · Account.
- Free tier: a 3-zone fall map with checkboxes, a per-zone "0/5 tasks complete" counter, an overall "Fall Cleaning Progress" bar and a Reset Progress button.
- Paid tier: an "Upgrade to VIP Access" banner at the bottom selling an 8-zone version.

Fall Essentials

Refresh Clean

## Lessons

- Asking a buyer for more money right after she scans the calendar's QR code costs trust when you most need it.
- Twice-a-year use is too infrequent to carry a paid tier.
- The VIP list was vague ("professional organizing strategies") and claimed expertise we can't back up. Don't reuse it.
- Reusable: the 3-zone structure, time estimates per zone and "transition + clean" split per zone make a good shape for a free seasonal checklist. Rewrite to [BRAND_VOICE.md](../../editorial/BRAND_VOICE.md) and add family-participation tasks (kids swap their own clothes, partner owns the garage/storage zone).
