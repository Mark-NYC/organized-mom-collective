## Page copy (transcribed verbatim)

### The Seasonal Family Table

*Finally, a meal planning system that works WITH your busy life, not against it.*

If you've ever stood in your kitchen at 5 PM wondering "what's for dinner?" you're not alone. Most families plan meal by meal, shopping trip by shopping trip, without any real strategy. The result? Food waste, decision fatigue, and constant scrambling.

### What Makes This System Different

Instead of planning individual meals, you think in **ingredient systems**. This isn't just another meal plan—it's a complete shift that builds confidence and saves your sanity.

**1. Choose Your Core 5**
Each week revolves around 5 versatile ingredients that appear in multiple meals. Think building blocks for the week.

**Example:** Ground turkey, sweet potatoes, white beans, coconut milk, and brown rice become:
- Monday: Turkey chili with beans
- Tuesday: Coconut curry with sweet potatoes
- Wednesday: Chili rice bowls
- Thursday: Sweet potato hash
- Friday: Curry over fresh rice

One ingredient list, five different meals.

**2. Shop Once, Cook Smart**
One organized shopping list covers your entire week, plus a simple Sunday prep routine saves 20+ minutes every weeknight.

**Your Sunday Power Hour:**
- Wash and chop vegetables
- Cook grains in bulk
- Prep proteins for quick cooking

**3. Follow the Flow**
Monday's chili becomes Tuesday's rice bowl and Wednesday's pasta sauce. Every leftover has a planned second life—no waste, no eating the same meal three nights straight.

**4. Build Kitchen Confidence**
After 4 weeks, you'll intuitively understand how ingredients work together. You'll create satisfying meals from what you have on hand.

### Why This Changes Everything

- **Budget:** Every shopping list item has multiple purposes.
- **Time:** Prep once Sunday, cook efficiently all week.
- **Stress:** Know what's for dinner with ingredients prepped.
- **Family:** More variety, better nutrition, meals that taste good.

### Getting Started

Each seasonal collection gives you 4 weeks of this strategic approach with core ingredient lists, transformation guides, organized shopping lists, and prep guides.

Start with one week. Follow the system exactly. Watch how different it feels to have a plan that actually works.

**This isn't about perfect meal planning. This is about sustainable meal planning.**

Ready to stop scrambling? Choose your first week below.
