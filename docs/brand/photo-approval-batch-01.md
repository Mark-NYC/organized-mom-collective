## Approval criteria

- Rate visual match (American lived-in home), naturalism, color consistency, and usable crop.
- Grade softly: warm-neutral whites, natural skin, gentle contrast, modest saturation. Do not batch-fix mismatched lighting with an aggressive filter.
- If house feels Scandinavian/editorial or image is dated, reject regardless of technical quality.
- Catalog verified source/license separately from subject release and commercial context. No customer endorsement implied.
- **Do not publish or represent real families as customers.** Free copyright license does not establish model releases, particularly for children.

## Proposed workflow

Select 4–6 favorites from this batch. Then source closely related photoshoots / color palettes. After selection, make a low-res contact sheet and grade previews on a matching white background. Save originals outside deployed assets, and only publish rights-cleared files.
