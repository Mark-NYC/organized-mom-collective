## Returns and refunds

We want you to love your calendar. If something isn't right, email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** with your
order number, or simply reply to your order confirmation email.

### Damaged, defective or incorrect items
If your calendar arrives damaged, has a printing or binding defect, or isn't what you ordered,
email us within **[14] days of delivery** with your order number and a photo. We'll send a
replacement or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer, at no cost to you. You won't need to
send anything back unless we ask, and if we do, we'll provide a prepaid return label.

### Changed your mind
You can return an **unused** calendar in its original condition within **14 days of delivery**.
Unused means not written in and without missing or torn pages.

1. Email us first so we can confirm your return and send you the return address.
2. Ship it back. Return shipping is paid by you, and we recommend a trackable service.
3. Once the calendar reaches us and we've checked it, we'll refund the price of the calendar
   [and the original shipping charge / but not the original shipping charge].

### Canceling before it ships
If your order hasn't shipped yet, email us to cancel it and we'll refund you in full, including
shipping. Once it has shipped, it's a return (above).

### When you'll get your money back
We'll issue your refund within **[3] business days** of approving it (for returns, after the
calendar reaches us). Refunds go back to your original payment method and usually take
5–10 business days to appear, depending on your bank. We'll email you when the refund is issued.

### The cleaning companion
The cleaning companion website comes free with your calendar and isn't sold separately, so it
isn't part of a refund.

Questions: **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**

## How this maps to the dashboard

- **Damaged or incorrect:** refund the order in the dashboard, or ship a replacement by hand. A replacement isn't a website order: take the unit out of stock with Inventory → *Add / remove* (reason "Replacement for #1003").
- **Change-of-mind return:** refund once the calendar arrives. On the order page, return it to stock **only if it's sellable**. A refund never puts units back on its own.
- **Canceled before shipping:** a full refund marks the order canceled. The calendars never left your shelf, so return them to stock from the order page.
