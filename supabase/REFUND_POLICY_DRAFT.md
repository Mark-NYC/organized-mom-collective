## Refunds and returns

We want you to love your calendar. If something's wrong, email us at **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** with your
order number and we'll make it right.

**Damaged or defective.** If your calendar arrives damaged or has a printing or binding defect,
email us within **[14] days of delivery** with your order number and a photo. We'll send a
replacement or give you a full refund, your choice. You don't need to send it back.

**Wrong item.** If we sent the wrong calendar or the wrong quantity, email us within **[14] days of
delivery**. We'll send what you ordered at no cost to you, and if we need the wrong item back,
we'll send you a prepaid return label.

**Changed your mind.** You can return an unused calendar in its original condition within
**[30] days of delivery** for a refund of the calendar price. Email us first and we'll tell you
where to send it. Return shipping is paid by you, and the original shipping charge isn't
refunded. Calendars that have been written in can't be returned.

**Canceling an order.** If your order hasn't shipped yet, email us and we'll cancel it and refund
you in full.

**Lost packages.** If tracking hasn't updated in **[10] business days**, or shows delivered but
you don't have it, email us and we'll work it out with you.

**When you'll get your money back.** We issue refunds to your original payment method within
**[3] business days** of approving them (for returns, once the calendar reaches us). Your bank
usually takes 5–10 business days to show it. You'll get an email when we issue the refund.

**The cleaning companion** is free with your calendar and isn't sold separately, so it isn't
part of the refund amount.

Questions: **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**

## How this maps to the dashboard

- Damaged / defective / wrong item: refund in the dashboard (full or partial), or ship a replacement by hand. A replacement isn't a new website order. Take the unit out of stock with Inventory → *Add / remove* (reason: "Replacement for #1001").
- Returns: refund when the calendar arrives, then return it to stock from the order page **only if it's sellable**. Refunds never put units back in stock on their own.
- Canceled before shipping: a full refund marks the order canceled. The calendars are still on your shelf, so return them to stock from the order page.
