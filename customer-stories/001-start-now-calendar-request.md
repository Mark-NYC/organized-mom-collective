# Customer Story 001: The Start-Now Request

**Date:** November 9, 2026  
**Product:** 2027 Family Wall Calendar (11 × 17 in)  
**Channel:** Etsy  
**Status:** Two complimentary 2026 monthly sheets ordered for local print pickup; pickup and customer reaction not yet confirmed.

> Internal marketing note. Customer is anonymized because this repository is public. Do not add identifiable screenshots, shipping details, order numbers, or a full name without explicit permission.

## What happened

An early calendar buyer received their order and reached out the same day to say they loved the layout. They asked whether they could purchase November and December 2026 pages so their family could use the system immediately rather than waiting until January 2027.

Those 2026 pages were not part of the printed calendar inventory. The founders decided to prepare two 11 × 17 in monthly sheets and pay for them to be printed at a Staples location convenient to the customer for pickup, as a thank-you. A Staples order confirmation was shown on November 9.

## Why this matters

- The buyer proactively requested *more* of the same product after delivery.
- They valued immediate use, revealing friction in a January-start calendar purchased in November.
- Local printing offered a low-cost way to solve an individual customer problem without creating new inventory.
- The response may build loyalty and word of mouth, but there is no review or testimonial yet.

## Potential experiment

Try a **Start Now** bonus for late-year calendar buyers: downloadable or locally printable current-year pages that bridge the gap to January. Track whether it improves conversion, customer satisfaction, and repeat purchases before making it standard.
